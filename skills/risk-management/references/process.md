# The risk management process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | risks are identified, analysed, treated and monitored continuously throughout the life cycle of a product or a service, the risks of its acquisition, development, maintenance and operation included | - |
| risks identified | the scope of risk management is defined, the risk management strategies are defined and carried out, and the risks are identified at the start and as they develop | the strategy and the context recorded; each risk the work met entered in the risk profile, or named to the user where the project keeps none |
| risks analysed | the risks are analysed, and the priority in which they receive resources is set | the likelihood, the consequences, the acceptable level and the priority of each risk recorded in the profile |
| responses selected | the response options are identified, prioritized and selected | for each risk above its acceptable level, the recommended responses with their effectiveness measures recorded, and the decision of the stakeholders who decide on risks on the risk action request |
| responses implemented | adequate response measures are implemented by the priority, likelihood and consequences of each risk, or the stakeholders who decide on risks accept the risk | the actions of the response tracked to closure; an accepted risk marked high priority in the profile and monitored |
| risk state measured | the risks are evaluated quantitatively to show the change of their state and the progress of the response | the profile updated on each change of state, and the effectiveness measures of the responses analysed |
