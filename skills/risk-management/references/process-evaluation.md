# Evaluating the risk management process

## Evaluating the risk management process

Outcomes served: risks identified, for the systemic risks; the other rules serve the improvement of
the process.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Information | shall collect information throughout the project's life cycle for improving the risk management process and for learning lessons; the knowledge management process distributes the lessons in the organization |
| Content | should name in that information the risks identified, their sources and causes, the response to each, and examples of successful responses |
| Review of the process | shall review the risk management process periodically for effectiveness and efficiency, and name the improvement opportunities it finds; the life cycle model management process changes the organization's process |
| Systemic risks | shall review the information on the risks identified and on the responses to them periodically, to reveal the systemic risks of the project and of the organization |
