# Analysing risks on purpose

## Analysing risks

Outcomes served: risks identified, risks analysed.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Start and course | shall identify the risks at the start of the project, within the project's strategy, and again as they develop, those found by the analysis of operation risks and by technical reviews included |
| Report | shall give the result as a risk analysis report: the risks identified, with their likelihood, their consequences and their priority |
| System studies | should look for risks in the studies of reliability, safety, performance and operation |
| Assessments | should look for risks in the assessments of technology, architecture and readiness, and in trade-off studies |
| Measures | should look for risks in the analysis of the system's measures |
| Life cycle | should look for risks at every stage, from the early ones to utilization, support and retirement |
| Project level | should cover at the level of the project the risks of cost, schedule, effort, resources and the technical risks |
