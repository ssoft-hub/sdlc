# Monitoring risks and reporting the profile

## Monitoring risks continuously

Outcomes served: risks identified, risk state measured.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Change of state | shall monitor the risks and the context for changes, and estimate anew a risk whose state has changed |
| Profile update | shall update the profile on each change of state |
| Points of reconciliation | should reconcile the profile with the work at the points the work touches it: a decision gate, a change of a condition an estimate rests on, work on the part a risk concerns |
| Decisions | should name, with an output presented at a decision gate or for another decision, the risks of the profile that concern that decision |
| New risks | shall monitor the emergence of new risks and of their sources throughout the life cycle |

## Responding to risks

Outcomes served: responses implemented.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Preventive measures | shall take preventive measures that reduce or avoid the impact of a risk whose expected reduction is not achieved |
| Recovery plan | shall plan, where mitigation cannot reduce or avoid that impact, corrective actions for the problem the risk results in, recorded as the recovery plan |
| New strategy | may take as a preventive measure a new response strategy or a change to an existing one |
| Changed response | shall treat a measure that changes the response the stakeholders who decide on risks selected as the selection of a response, an act of `SKILL.md` |

## Managing the risk profile

Outcomes served: risk state measured.

The agent shall communicate the risk profile, or the part each stakeholder needs, to the
stakeholders periodically and according to their needs.
