## Planning risk management

Outcomes served: risks identified.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Strategy | shall define the scope of risk management and the strategy: the policy - the guidelines that govern risk management - and the strategies and measures by which each risk or set of risks, at the level of the project and of the organization, is identified, analysed, treated and monitored |
| Strategy record | shall record the strategy in the risk management plan once the decision that sets it is taken |
| Supply chain | should cover in the strategy the risk management of every supplier of the supply chain, and state how their risks are escalated into the project's risks |
| Description of the process | shall record the description of the project's risk management process, with how the process is evaluated and improved |
| Lessons in the evaluation | should provide in that description for accumulating knowledge and experience |
| Parties responsible | shall define the parties responsible for risk management, their roles and their duties |
| Resources | shall provide those parties with sufficient resources, which the project planning process allocates |
| Context | shall define and record the context of risk management: the stakeholders' perspectives, the risk categories, the technical and management goals, the assumptions and the constraints, each possibly by reference |
| Categories and completeness | should cover by the risk categories the technical areas of the system, and build the list of risks from the events that can create, enhance, prevent, degrade, accelerate or delay the achievement of the goals, aiming at a complete list |
