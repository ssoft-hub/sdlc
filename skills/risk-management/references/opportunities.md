# Opportunities

## Planning risk management

Outcomes served: risks identified.

The agent may handle an opportunity by this skill. The agent should link each opportunity pursued
to the risks that diminish its expected benefit. The agent should consider the risk of underusing
the opportunity and the risk of not achieving its effect.

## Analysing risks

Outcomes served: responses selected.

The agent may count among the response strategies for a risk linked to an opportunity accepting or
increasing that risk for the sake of the opportunity.
