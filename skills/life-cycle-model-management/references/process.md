# The life cycle model management process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | the policy, the life cycle processes, the life cycle models and the procedures are defined, maintained and available for use by the organization: consistent with its goals, tailorable to the needs of a project, and capable of improvement | - |
| rules established | the life cycle policy, processes, models and procedures are established | the policy and the procedures for managing the project's rules, the standard set of processes and the standard life cycle models, recorded and in effect |
| authority defined | responsibility, accountability and authority within them are defined | the roles recorded - who performs, who checks, who decides - each with its responsibility, accountability and authority |
| processes maintained | the life cycle processes, life cycle models and procedures are defined and maintained for use by the organization | each process described where the project names, the tailoring guideline, the data on the use of the processes, and the records of reviews and assessments |
| improvements implemented | improvements to the processes, life cycle models and procedures are implemented by justified priorities | each improvement put into effect by a decision with its priority and its reason, communicated, and its effect confirmed against its goal |
