# Establishing the processes

## Establishing the processes

Outcomes served: rules established, authority defined, processes maintained.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Policy and procedures | shall establish and record the policy and the procedures for managing and deploying the project's rules as the process strategy, consistent with the organization's strategy and goals: who establishes, changes, puts into effect and cancels a project rule, and how |
| Standard processes | shall establish a standard set of processes for all the processes and life cycle models that the project applies: the purpose of each process, where it applies, and the interactions between the processes, with the quality policy that the quality management process sets put into effect in them |
| Conformance | shall have the processes it establishes implement the requirements of ISO/IEC/IEEE 12207:2017, a provision it leaves out tailored by `references/tailoring.md` |
| Description | shall describe each standard process: its tasks, its activities, the work products related to it and its expected performance characteristics |
| Place | shall record the processes and their application in specific cases in a place the project names |
| Roles | shall define, record and maintain the roles, the responsibility, the accountability and the authority: who performs, who checks, and who decides - accepts an output, passes a stage's decision gate, approves a change to a rule |
| Management mechanism | should establish, where needed, a mechanism for managing the processes - their development, monitoring, control and improvement -, with its duties and authority |
| Business criteria | shall define the business criteria for managing progression through the life cycle: the criteria for the decisions on entry into and exit from each stage and on the major milestones |
| Life cycle models | shall establish and record the standard life cycle models in the form of the model of `software-life-cycle`: the stages, each with its purpose and outputs, the processes it draws on, and the decisions that open and close it |
| Overlap | shall state in a life cycle model it establishes where stages overlap or repeat according to scale, complexity and changing needs |
| Tailoring guideline | shall establish a guideline for tailoring the standard processes to the specific needs of a project, a product or a service |
