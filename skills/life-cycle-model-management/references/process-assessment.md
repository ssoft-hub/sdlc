# Process assessment

The assessment sponsor is whoever in the organization commissions the assessment; those assessed
are the projects and the parts of the organization whose processes it covers.

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| adopting an assessment plan | - | Commitment |

## Assessing the processes

Outcomes served: processes maintained.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Procedure | should develop, record and apply a process assessment procedure, whose records of assessments the row "Record" below governs at its force |
| Goals | shall define the assessment goals and check them against the organization's business goals |
| Criteria | shall define criteria for checking that the assessment goals are achieved |
| Plan | shall develop and record the assessment plan |
| Commitment | shall obtain, before the assessment, the commitment of the assessment sponsor and of those assessed to the assessment plan, the agreed schedule and resources included |
| Data | shall perform the assessment by collecting the data within its scope |
| Sufficiency | shall check that the collected data covers the purpose of the assessment sufficiently |
| Analysis | shall analyse the checked data and name the strengths and weaknesses of the processes |
| Results | shall communicate the assessment results to the assessment sponsor in an assessment report |
| Record | shall keep the assessment record, with accurate and current results, in an accessible place and format - the assessment results repository -, and record its backup and recovery |
