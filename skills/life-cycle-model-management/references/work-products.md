# Work products of life cycle model management

The outcomes are those `references/process.md` names. Where this process is both the source and
the receiver, the work product passes from one task of life cycle model management to a later one,
or is the version in force that the process updates. The assessment sponsor and those assessed
are roles in the organization (`references/process-assessment.md`), not processes; "all life
cycle processes" means every process of the life cycle takes or gives the work product.
