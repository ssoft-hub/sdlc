# Tailoring

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| the decision on tailoring the processes of the standard, also for a project that applies the standard directly | - | Circumstances, Criticality, Inputs, Selection |

Selecting the life cycle model and the processes of the project is an act of the project planning
process.

## Establishing the processes

Outcomes served: rules established, processes maintained.

### Tailoring the processes of the standard

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Need | should not treat tailoring as required for conformance |
| Additions | should not treat a process with outcomes, activities and tasks beyond those the standard requires as needing tailoring |
| Circumstances | shall identify and record the circumstances that influence the tailoring: the stability and variety of the operational environment, the commercial and operational risks of the parties, novelty, scale and complexity, the start and the duration of operation, integrity - safety, security, privacy, usability, suitability -, emerging technologies, the budget and the resources, the services of enabling systems, the roles and the authority, and conformance to other standards |
| Criticality | shall take into account, for properties critical to the system, the life cycle structures that standards on criticality recommend or prescribe |
| Inputs | shall obtain inputs from the parties that take the tailoring decision: the stakeholders of the system, the stakeholders of the organization's agreements, and the organization's functions concerned, for the tailoring decision, an act of this file whose method the decision management process provides |
| Selection | shall select the processes that require tailoring and delete the outcomes, activities and tasks not applied, so that the modified or new processes achieve the purposes and outcomes of the life cycle model |
| Full conformance | should delete no provision of the standard where full conformance is claimed: a deleted provision makes the conformance tailored |
| Deletion | should delete a provision of the standard rather than rewrite it, with regard to the consequences for the other processes |

### Tailoring the organization's rules for a project

The agent should tailor the organization's policy, life cycle models and procedures to a project's
requirements - the complexity of the work, the methods, the skills - within the organization's
policy and by the tailoring guideline that `references/establishing.md` establishes. The agent
should tailor the processes of the standard that a project applies directly by the rules of the
subsection above.
