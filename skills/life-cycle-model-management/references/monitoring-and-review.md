# Monitoring and reviewing the processes

## Establishing the processes

Outcomes served: processes maintained.

The agent shall obtain and maintain the information and the data on the use of the standard
processes in projects, the quality records included. The agent shall pass the analysis of that
data to the project planning process in an analysis report.

## Assessing the processes

Outcomes served: processes maintained, improvements implemented.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Monitoring | shall monitor the execution of the processes in all the projects of the organization; in a single-owner project, the project's own processes |
| Measures and feedback | should analyse in it the process measures - collected and analysed by the measurement process - and their trends against the business criteria of `references/establishing.md`, and the feedback from the work on the processes' effectiveness |
| Process reviews | shall plan, hold and record process reviews at the intervals the organization defines |
| Analysis | shall analyse in a review the life cycle model and the processes that each project applies: their suitability, adequacy and effectiveness in the light of the assessment results, and whether the stages, the processes and the criteria allow progression to be managed |
| Improvements named | shall name the improvements the results of a review call for, as improvement opportunities |
| Historical data | should accumulate and analyse historical data, technical data and evaluation data to understand the strengths and weaknesses of the processes |
| Use of the analysis | should use that analysis as feedback for process improvement, for recommendations to current and following projects, and for determining the need for new technologies |

## Improving the processes

Outcomes served: improvements implemented.

The agent should accumulate, maintain and use data on the cost of quality to improve the
organization's processes. Where that data is kept, the agent shall establish from it the costs of
prevention and of resolving problems and nonconformities in software products and services.
