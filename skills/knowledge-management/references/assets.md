# Making an asset reusable

The asset criteria, the asset classification scheme and the storage and retrieval mechanism are
those of `references/strategy.md`.

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| accepting an asset into the asset repository | the asset has not passed the acceptance or certification criteria | Submission, Record |

## Distributing knowledge assets

Outcomes served: knowledge developed, knowledge available.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Development | shall develop or acquire knowledge assets, the training materials and the architecture and design patterns, artifacts, reference architectures and standards that architecture and design definition give for reuse included |
| Way of obtaining | may obtain an asset by acquisition or by development; acquiring one from outside the organization is carried by the acquisition process |
| Record | shall record and classify each asset |
| Evaluation | shall evaluate each asset by the acceptance and certification procedures |
| Review | shall have each asset reviewed with developers and asset managers |
| Handover | shall hand each asset over to the asset manager for use in products |
| Submission | shall evaluate an asset submitted for storage against the acceptance and certification criteria |
| Acceptance | shall certify an accepted asset, classify it, baseline it, enter it in the software assets register or, for hardware, the hardware resources register, and make it available through the storage and retrieval mechanism |
| Proposal | shall evaluate each proposal to take an asset into a product for the asset's suitability to that use, and return it with the evaluation to the process applying the asset - architecture definition, implementation -, whose decision it is |
