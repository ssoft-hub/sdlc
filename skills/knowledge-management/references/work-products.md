# Work products of knowledge management

The outcomes are those `references/process.md` names. Where this process is both the source and the receiver, the
work product is the version in force that the process updates, or passes from one task of knowledge
management to a later one. The users of an asset, the experts and users of knowledge, and the
participants of the reuse program are roles in the organization, not processes. "All life cycle
processes" means every process of the life cycle takes the work product: what is distributed across
the organization reaches each of them.
