# Domain engineering

A domain is an area in which systems or products have significant common and varying properties;
domain engineering creates assets for its class of systems, subsystems or applications. Change
requests for domain models and architectures follow `references/assets-in-use.md`.

## Distributing knowledge assets

Outcomes served: taxonomy defined, knowledge developed.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Plan | shall plan domain engineering and carry it out by the plan |
| Forms | shall select the forms of representation, the classification and the description templates of the domain's models and architectures by the organization's reuse standards, and record them in the domain representation standard |
| Procedures | shall define the procedures for receiving and resolving problems and change requests for assets, and for feedback to the asset manager |
| Concurrency | may conduct domain engineering alongside the development and maintenance that use its assets |
| Boundaries | shall identify the boundaries of the domain and its relationships with other domains |
| Needs | shall identify the current and anticipated needs of the stakeholders of the domain's products |
| Domain models | shall create, in the selected forms, the domain models: the common and varying properties, capabilities, concepts and functions |
| Vocabulary | shall compile a vocabulary of the domain's terms and of the relationships between similar or common assets |
| Models recorded | shall classify and record the domain models |
| Models evaluated | shall evaluate the models and the vocabulary by the selected modelling technique and the asset acceptance and certification procedures |
| Models reviewed | shall have the models reviewed with developers, asset managers, domain experts and users |
| Models handed over | shall hand the domain models over to the asset manager |
| Domain architecture | shall create and record the domain architecture - the family of systems of the domain, their commonality and variability -, consistent with the domain model and the organization's standards, its technical interfaces with other domains recorded in the domain interface specification |
| Architecture evaluated | shall evaluate it by the architecture design technique and the acceptance and certification procedures |
| Architecture reviewed | shall have the domain design reviewed with developers, domain experts and asset managers |
| Architecture handed over | shall hand the domain architecture over to the asset manager |
| Asset specifications | shall develop and record an asset specification for each object selected for reuse, and maintain it as the domain design changes |
| Specifications evaluated | shall evaluate each by the acceptance and certification procedures |
| Work of other processes | may develop the domain architecture by the work of system/software requirements definition, and an asset specification by that of system/software requirements definition or architecture definition |
