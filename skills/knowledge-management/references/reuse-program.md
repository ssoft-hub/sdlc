# The reuse program

Accepting an asset is an act of `references/assets.md`.

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| adopting or changing the reuse strategy or the reuse plan - the program implementation plan -, the plan's acceptance by the steering function and the managers included | - | for the strategy, Start; for the plan, Its evaluation |
| assigning the sponsor and the roles and functions of the program | - | Sponsor, Participants and roles, Steering function, Support function |

## Planning knowledge management

Outcomes served: strategy defined, knowledge developed.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Start | shall establish and record, for the reuse program, the organization's reuse strategy - its purpose, scope, ultimate and intermediate goals - with the reuse policy the projects follow in applying assets |
| Program | shall define the reuse program and its supporting infrastructure |
| Sponsor | shall name the reuse sponsor |
| Participants and roles | shall identify the participants of the program and their roles |
| Steering function | shall establish a steering function, with authority and duties for the program |
| Support function | shall establish a support function for the program |
| Domains | shall identify and record, with the appropriate manager, the domain engineers, the users and the software developers, the domains in which to explore reuse or carry out the organization's intention to practise it: sets of systems and components with common properties that can be organized into a collection of assets |
| Their evaluation | shall evaluate the domains for an accurate reflection of the reuse strategy |
| Their review | shall have them reviewed with software developers, domain engineers and users taking part |
| Their refinement | may refine a domain and review its scope when new information on the domains or the organization's plans appears, or a domain analysis is conducted |
| Readiness | shall assess the organization's readiness and capability for systematic reuse - the bases and success criteria of the program - and record the result in the assessment report, or in the audit report where an audit makes the assessment |
| Domain potential | shall assess the reuse potential of each domain considered |
| Recommendations | shall recommend, on those assessments, how to refine the reuse strategy and the reuse plan |
| Infrastructure | shall improve the reuse infrastructure incrementally - skills, technologies, processes, organizational structure, indicators - with the acquirers, suppliers, developers, operators, maintainers, asset managers and domain engineers |
| Implementation plan | shall create, record and maintain the reuse plan: the program implementation plan, with its resources and procedures |
| Its evaluation | shall evaluate the plan for completeness, feasibility and the ability to implement the reuse strategy |
| Steering in the evaluation | should involve representatives of the steering function in it |
| Acceptance of the plan | shall have the plan accepted by the steering function and the managers |
| Its review | shall have the plan reviewed with representatives of the steering function and the managers taking part |

## Managing knowledge, skills and knowledge assets

Outcomes served: knowledge developed, knowledge available, use analysed.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Running | shall perform the activities of the reuse plan by the plan |
| Progress | shall track the program's progress against the reuse strategy, and adjust the plan by it |
| Problems | shall register and resolve the problems and nonconformities of the program |
| Support | shall periodically confirm management's financial support and commitments to the program |
| Communication | shall establish feedback, assessment, communication and notification between the participants |
| Participants | should count among them, where the project has them, the program administrators, asset managers, domain engineers, developers, operators and maintainers |
| Assessment | shall periodically assess the program for achieving the reuse strategy, for its suitability to current needs and for its effectiveness, and record the results in the reuse evaluation report |
| Lessons | shall gather and analyse the lessons of projects and related processes, and keep them in the process repository |
| Results | shall present the results of the assessments and the lessons to the steering function and the managers |
| Changes | shall recommend changes to the program on those results, and make the changes decided |
