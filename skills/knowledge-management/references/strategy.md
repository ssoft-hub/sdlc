# The strategy and the means of knowledge management

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| adopting or changing the knowledge management strategy, the asset management strategy or the asset management plan | - | Strategy, Requirements for assets, Asset management strategy, Asset management plan, Review of the plan - each for what is adopted |
| adopting or changing the classification of knowledge and skills, the taxonomy, the asset classification scheme, or the criteria of acceptance, certification and retirement of assets | - | Classification, Taxonomy, Asset classification scheme, Asset criteria - each for what is adopted |

## Planning knowledge management

Outcomes served: strategy defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Strategy | shall define and record the knowledge management strategy on the basis of the needs of the organization, its people, its domains and its projects |
| Requirements for assets | shall plan in the strategy the requirements for managing knowledge assets: the infrastructure, the training of their contributors and users - given by human resource management -, the classification scheme and the asset criteria |
| Content | should include in the strategy the plans for obtaining and keeping knowledge, skills and assets for their useful life; the types gathered and kept; the criteria for accepting, qualifying and removing them; the procedures for managing their change; the plans, mechanisms and procedures for protecting classified and important information and for access to it; the storage and retrieval mechanisms |
| Outside the organization | should cover in the strategy the knowledge shared with stakeholders, acquirers and business partners |
| Asset management strategy | shall define and record an asset management strategy for reuse |
| Asset management plan | shall draw up and record an asset management plan defining the resources and procedures of asset management |
| Carrying it out | shall carry out the asset management plan |
| Review of the plan | shall have the plan reviewed with domain engineers and program administrators taking part |
| Asset criteria | shall define and record the criteria for the acceptance, certification and retirement of assets |

## Distributing knowledge and skills

Outcomes served: taxonomy defined, knowledge developed, knowledge available.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Classification | shall establish, record and maintain a classification for obtaining and sharing knowledge and skills across the organization |
| Its scope | should cover in it the knowledge of experts, common knowledge, knowledge in special areas, and skills |
| Experts | shall set up and keep current a network of experts - who they are, their areas, what the classification scheme holds on them - and establish their interaction |
| Knowledge management system | shall create and maintain the knowledge repository, for gathering and sharing experience and best practices, with a mechanism for identifying, classifying, sharing and using knowledge between experts and projects, with access, storage and retrieval; the tool it runs on is an infrastructure element, which infrastructure management obtains and maintains |
| Information | shall gather, while the knowledge management strategy is in force, the information that it gives the organization access to, and maintain that information |
| Organizational learning | shall create the mechanisms of organizational learning when the knowledge repository is set up, and manage them while it is in use, so that the common knowledge stays current and useful for future projects |

## Distributing knowledge assets

Outcomes served: taxonomy defined, knowledge available.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Taxonomy | shall establish and record a taxonomy organizing the knowledge assets |
| Its content | should define in it the boundaries of areas and their relationships, the models of areas with significant common and varying properties - capabilities, concepts, functions -, and the architecture of a family of systems within an area |
| Asset classification scheme | shall develop, record and maintain it |
| Its classes | should distinguish in it, where the organization has them, commercially available and custom software, commercially available hardware, and reusable software elements |
| Distribution | shall distribute knowledge assets among experts, users and projects |
| Storage and retrieval | shall implement and maintain the storage and retrieval mechanism of the asset repository and the reuse library, available for storing and retrieving assets and information on their reusability |
| Operation | shall ensure and monitor the work of the asset repository, of retrieval and of distribution |
| Review of the mechanism | shall have the mechanism reviewed with program administrators and domain engineers taking part |
