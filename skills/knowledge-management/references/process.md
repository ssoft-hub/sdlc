# The knowledge management process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | the capabilities and assets are created that enable the organization to reuse existing knowledge, so that the knowledge, information and skills of people are accumulated, shared, reused and improved throughout the organization; the process covers knowledge, skills and knowledge assets, those relating to system elements included; for reuse, domain models, domain architectures and assets for domains are developed and maintained, reusable assets are managed from concept to retirement, and the organization's reuse program is planned, established, managed, controlled and monitored | - |
| strategy defined | a knowledge management strategy is defined | the knowledge management strategy, the asset management strategy and the reuse strategy recorded where the project names, each adopted by a decision |
| taxonomy defined | a taxonomy for the application of knowledge assets is defined | the classification of knowledge and skills, the taxonomy and the asset classification scheme recorded |
| knowledge developed | knowledge, skills and knowledge assets are developed or acquired | each knowledge item recorded by the classification scheme; each asset recorded, evaluated against the acceptance and certification criteria and accepted by a decision |
| knowledge available | knowledge, skills and knowledge assets are available to the organization | the knowledge repository and the asset repository in use through their storage and retrieval mechanism, each accepted asset baselined under configuration management, and the users of an asset notified of each change to it |
| use analysed | data on the use of the knowledge management process are gathered and analysed | the use data of knowledge, skills and knowledge assets recorded, with their analysis |
