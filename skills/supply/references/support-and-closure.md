# Support, payment, transfer and closure

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| the transfer of responsibility | a criterion not passed; a date that cannot be met; an action the agreement forbids; an open item - a duty of either party that has fallen due and is not met; what can be checked is checked; a release criterion whose fulfilment cannot be shown counts as unmet; a condition that can be checked before the act but is not confirmed counts as unmet, and the act waits; a condition that can be checked only at acceptance is named and left to the acceptance checks | Transfer, Record of transfer |
| closure | a criterion not passed; a date that cannot be met; an action the agreement forbids; an open item - a duty of either party that has fallen due and is not met; what can be checked is checked; a release criterion whose fulfilment cannot be shown counts as unmet; a condition that can be checked before the act but is not confirmed counts as unmet, and the act waits; a condition that can be checked only at acceptance is named and left to the acceptance checks | Closure, Open items, Record of closure, Portfolio |

## Delivering and supporting the product or the service

Outcomes served: obligations satisfied, responsibility transferred.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Support | shall assist the acquirer in the support of the delivered product in the agreed type, level and duration |
| Beyond the terms | shall treat a request for support beyond the agreed type, level and duration as a request to change the agreement, by `references/agreement.md` |
| Published product | shall treat a request for support of a published product beyond its published terms instead as one the project takes on or rejects by a decision, the published terms unchanged |
| Payment | shall confirm to the acquirer the receipt of payment or of the other agreed action; for a published product, only where its published terms require payment |
| Transfer | shall transfer responsibility for the product or the service as the agreement directs: to the acquirer or to a party the agreement names |
| Record of transfer | shall record the transfer: what was transferred - source code, access, operation -, to whom and when |
| Published product's transfer | shall transfer no responsibility to a named party for a published product: its licence conditions state what its user takes on |
| Obligations left | should name the obligations left with the project after the transfer, such as a warranty period; for a published product, only where its published terms give a warranty |
| Closure | shall close the agreement once the deliveries are accepted, the payment is confirmed, the transfer is done and no open item remains |
| Open items | shall name each item that keeps the agreement open |
| Record of closure | shall pass the record of closure of the agreement to the acquirer |
| Licence | shall not close an agreement that is the licence of a published product while the product is distributed or supported |
| Portfolio | should pass the record of closure to the portfolio management process, which closes the project |
