# Responding to a request

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| the choice of a supply strategy option | a criterion not passed; a date that cannot be met; an action the agreement forbids; an open item - a duty of either party that has fallen due and is not met; what can be checked is checked; a release criterion whose fulfilment cannot be shown counts as unmet; a condition that can be checked before the act but is not confirmed counts as unmet, and the act waits; a condition that can be checked only at acceptance is named and left to the acceptance checks | Options, Recommendation, Named option |
| the decision to respond to a request or to accept a contract the acquirer proposed; for a published product, to take a request on | a criterion not passed; a date that cannot be met; an action the agreement forbids; an open item - a duty of either party that has fallen due and is not met; what can be checked is checked; a release criterion whose fulfilment cannot be shown counts as unmet; a condition that can be checked before the act but is not confirmed counts as unmet, and the act waits; a condition that can be checked only at acceptance is named and left to the acceptance checks | Review, Unclear requirement |
| sending the response, and a promise of a price or a date | a criterion not passed; a date that cannot be met; an action the agreement forbids; an open item - a duty of either party that has fallen due and is not met; what can be checked is checked; a release criterion whose fulfilment cannot be shown counts as unmet; a condition that can be checked before the act but is not confirmed counts as unmet, and the act waits; a condition that can be checked only at acceptance is named and left to the acceptance checks | Review, Unclear requirement, Estimates, Answers, Price and date |

## Preparing for the supply

Outcomes served: response given.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Acquirer | shall name the acquirer of the product or the service and its need; for a product made for many acquirers, the market and its need |
| Options | shall define the supply strategy before a request is evaluated, by comparing by risk the options - in-house, under contract, buying off-the-shelf, a combination of these |
| Recommendation | shall recommend one option and leave the choice to the user |
| Named option | shall name the risks of the option the user's explicit request names, instead of the comparison |
| Provisional | shall mark the recommendation provisional where information the comparison needs is missing, and name what would change it |
| Review on risk | shall review a provisional recommendation on a risk analysis once the agreement and the requirements for the plans are known |

## Responding to a request

Outcomes served: response given, obligations satisfied.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Channel | shall maintain a channel for the acquirer's requests from the first request on, recording the communication with the acquirer in it |
| Review | shall review the request, before any commitment, under the organization's policies: its source is genuine, the kind of product is clear, each requirement is defined and feasible under the chosen or recommended strategy |
| Published information | shall also review, for a published product, its published documentation, licence and terms of support against the request |
| Handler | shall name who in the project handles the request - a role is enough - in the response, or in the report to the user where no response goes out |
| Feasibility study | shall name the need for a feasibility study where a firm price or date cannot be given |
| Earlier requirements | should resolve each difference between a requirement of the request and an earlier one |
| Review results | should record the results of the review |
| Unclear requirement | shall confirm with the acquirer, and record as confirmed, each requirement that is unwritten or unclear: one admitting readings that differ in cost, date or acceptance |
| Own reading | shall place the project's own reading of an unwritten or unclear requirement, where the response answers with it, among the assumptions to be confirmed, not in the agreed scope of work |
| Criteria | shall establish criteria for evaluating a request from the organization's business goals |
| Evaluation | shall evaluate the request against the criteria of "Criteria" - whether to respond, whether to accept a contract the acquirer proposed, its content checked by `references/agreement.md`, or for a published product whether to take the request on -, record the evaluation and leave the decision to the user |
| Estimates | shall estimate the cost, the resources and the delivery date before a response offering work; for a published product, only for a request the project takes on |
| Performers | shall identify the performers with the competence each part offered needs, an option included, by their roles |
| Uncovered part | shall name how a part no one in the project can do is provided for: a subcontractor, a hire |
| Answers | shall answer each requirement of the request in the response: a defined one by what is offered for it, an unclear one as "Unclear requirement" and "Own reading" state |
| Price and date | shall give in the response a price and a date with the assumptions they rest on |
| Unmet requirement | shall name in the response each requirement the project cannot meet |
| Published product | shall give in the response of a published product, in place of the price and the date, a date only where the project binds itself to one |
| Regulations | should cover in the response the regulatory and legal requirements |

Assigning a person to a part of the work is an act of the human resource management process.
