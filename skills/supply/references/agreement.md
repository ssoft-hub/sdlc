## Establishing and maintaining the agreement

Outcomes served: agreement established.

The agent shall apply the next two tables to an agreement the parties negotiate: a contract, an
order, a subcontract. The agent shall apply to a published product's licence the row of the
licences of the third-party components the product includes.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Achievability | should check, before the project agrees, that the requirements, the milestones and the acceptance conditions are achievable, the control procedures - progress reporting, agreeing a change - workable, and the payment terms acceptable |
| Content | shall check, before the project agrees, that the agreement, whichever party drafted it, carries each item the table below marks shall, and record the result in the contract review record |
| Further terms | should settle in the agreement each item the table below marks should |
| Negotiation | should discuss each problem found during negotiation and have the changes made before signing |
| Signature | should have the agreement signed by a person authorized to sign it |
| Oral understanding | should record an oral understanding |
| Confirmation | shall have the agreement approved and formally confirmed |

| The agreement carries | Force |
|---|---|
| the requirements | shall |
| the deliverables and their dates | shall |
| the acceptance criteria | shall |
| the price and the payment terms | shall |
| the method of delivery, packaging, distribution and, where needed, installation | shall |
| the terms of support, maintenance and operation | shall |
| the duties of both parties | shall |
| the mechanism for monitoring the progress and the quality of the project's work | shall |
| the measures for the identified risks | shall |
| the licences of the third-party components the product includes | shall |
| the warranty | should |
| the intellectual property rights | should |
| the dates and the authority for starting closure | should |
| the acquisition logistics - support, spare parts, scheduled servicing -, where the maintenance of the product requires it | should |

### A change to the agreement

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Request beyond it | shall treat a request beyond the agreement as a request to change it |
| Own need | shall name a change the project itself needs - from a deviation found in checking the deliverables by `references/carrying-out.md`, from a condition that cannot be achieved - and propose it to the acquirer |
| Market | should consider a change to any section of the agreement also where a changing market calls for one |
| Impact | shall evaluate, before the project agrees to a change, its impact on the cost, the dates, the quality, the duties of both parties and the project's plans, stating "none" for each it leaves as it is |
| Published product | shall name, before a change of a published product's licence or terms of support, its impact on the product's users |
| Made | shall not treat a change as made until both parties have agreed it - for a published product, until the decision on it is taken - and the agreement records it |
| Record | shall treat a message as the agreement's record of a change only where the agreement is informal and the acquirer confirmed the change in that message |
| Affected documents | shall name, once a change is agreed, each document and plan it affects - the requirements, the schedule, the test plan, the release content - and whether each needs a correction |
| Communication | should communicate the change to those it concerns |
