# The supply process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | the acquirer is provided with a product or a service that meets the agreed requirements | - |
| response given | a response to the acquirer's request is given | the response sent to the acquirer, answering each requirement with what is offered, a price and a date and the assumptions they rest on; for a published product, the decision on the request told to the one who raised it |
| agreement established | an agreement is established between the acquirer and the supplier | the agreement accepted by both parties: a contract, a record of an oral understanding, or the licence and the published terms of support of a published product |
| product provided | a product or a service is provided | the release delivered with a confirmation of receipt, or published with its version visible in its channel |
| obligations satisfied | the supplier's obligations under the agreement are satisfied | the support given as agreed, the payment confirmed to the acquirer, and the record of closure with no open item left; for a published product, the support given as its published terms state |
| responsibility transferred | responsibility for the product or the service is transferred as the agreement directs | the record of transfer: what, to whom and when; for a published product, the licence conditions that state what its user takes on |
