# Carrying out an agreement

For a published product, the agent shall not apply the rows "Access", "Adaptation",
"Installation", "Preparing for use" and "Acceptance": no acquirer works with the project on them.

## Carrying out the agreement

Outcomes served: product provided, obligations satisfied.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Life cycle model | shall define or select, where the agreement does not set it, a life cycle model fit for the scope, size and complexity of the project: its stages, the goals and outputs of each, and the processes, activities and tasks selected and mapped onto them |
| Plans | shall carry out the agreement by the project's plans and by the requirements and the criteria of the agreement |
| Checks | shall check each deliverable's technical characteristics, costs, dates, progress and quality against the agreement continually, and keep the progress status record |
| Deviations | shall record each deviation or problem with an action named for it |
| Duties | shall check that every party meets its duties under the agreement, what the acquirer owes included |
| Communication | should communicate a deviation, a risk and their impact to the acquirer |
| Feedback | should receive, record and consider the acquirer's feedback and complaints |

### What the agreement defines

Outcomes served: obligations satisfied.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Acquirer's processes | should apply the acquirer's processes where the agreement so defines |
| Subcontractor | shall carry the requirements of the agreement on a part given to a subcontractor - a part the project acquires by the acquisition process - into the agreement with the subcontractor |
| Meetings and audits | shall take part in the meetings, the analysis of acceptance conditions and the audits - conducted by quality assurance - that the agreement and the project's plans define, and keep the agenda and the minutes of each meeting |
| Joint reviews | shall hold the joint reviews with the acquirer the agreement defines and record each |
| Other parties | shall work with an independent verification, validation or testing organization and the other parties as the agreement and the project's plans define |
| Coordination | should coordinate with the acquirer the review of the activities, the relationships and the communications the agreement provides for |
| Reports | shall make the reports on assessments, reviews, audits, tests and problem resolution available to the acquirer as the agreement defines |
| Access | shall give the acquirer access to the facilities of the project and of its subcontractors for reviews of the product or the service, as the agreement and the project's plans define |
| Acquirer's property | should identify the data, tools and software the acquirer provides, check them for suitability and protect them |
| Loss or damage | should report a loss of or damage to what the acquirer provided to the acquirer and record it |
| Use | should use what the acquirer provided only as the agreement allows |

## Delivering and supporting the product or the service

Outcomes served: product provided.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Adaptation | shall have the product adapted to the acquirer's environment and evaluated there in parallel with the existing systems or processes, before acceptance testing is passed, work the transition process does |
| Installation | shall install the product where the agreement requires, and record the installation |
| Preparing for use | shall give the acquirer the support and the training it needs in preparing for use and starting it, a duty of supply whose training work the transition process does |
| Acceptance | shall assist the acquirer in the review of the release, held as a joint review with the acquirer, and in acceptance testing, the project's part of the acceptance test plan included |
