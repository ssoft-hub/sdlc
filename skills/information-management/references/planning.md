## Preparing for information management

Outcomes served: information defined, representations defined, information handled.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Strategy | shall define the information management strategy - what is documented, in which part of the organization and at which stages of the life cycle of the product or the service -, taking it from the project's rules, naming what is missing and proposing a place for it |
| Views of one subject | should provide in the strategy for information on one subject being developed differently, at different stages and for different users, such as an interface description for a developer and for a user |
| Items | shall define the items managed during the life cycle and those retained for a defined period after its end, and the documents the life cycle of the product produces |
| Retention | should set the retention period of an item after the end of the system's life by the organization's policy, the agreements or the legislation |
| Documentation plan | shall develop, record and carry out a documentation plan naming for each document its title, its purpose and content, its users, the procedures and the responsibility for its source data, development, review, change, approval, production, storage, distribution, maintenance and configuration management, and the schedule of its intermediate and final versions |
| Configuration controls | shall establish for documents the controls the configuration management process requires |
| Source data under control | should place the source data and the transformation tools of a document, with the documentation they produce, under configuration management |
| Authority | shall assign the authority and the duties for the origination, creation, accumulation, archiving and use of the items, and define the rights, duties and obligations for their storage, transfer and access |
| Duties under the law | should inform those who work with information of their duties under the legislation on data, security and privacy: ownership, the restrictions of an agreement, access rights, the protection of confidential, proprietary and personal information, intellectual property |
| Representation | shall define the content, semantics, formats and structure of the items and the means of their representation, storage, transfer and retrieval |
| Standards | shall establish the standards for developing, changing and keeping documents, and the requirements for a document: format, title, date, identifier, version history, authors, reviewer, approver, content, purpose, distribution list |
| Constraints | should take into account the forms and the media of information and the constraints of the organization - its infrastructure, the links between organizations, distributed work -, and apply information standards and conventions by the policy, the agreements and the legislation |
| Maintenance actions | shall define the information maintenance actions |
| Content of maintenance | should include in them the analysis of the status of stored information - integrity, validity, availability, suitability - and, where needed, its copying or migration to another medium or format when technologies change, or the preservation of the tools that read the archive |
