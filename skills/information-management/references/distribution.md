# Distributing information

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| publishing or passing information outside the project or to those whom the strategy has not yet designated as receivers - the acquirer, everyone -, official documentation included | the receiver is not authorized for information with a restriction; the document has not passed the review or approval that the strategy requires; the source of the source data or the right to use them is not confirmed; a condition that can be checked before the act but is not confirmed counts as unmet; a condition that can be checked only at acceptance is named and left to the acceptance checks | Source data, Examination and Approval of `SKILL.md`; Delivery; Distribution record |
| disclosing information marked as confidential, proprietary or personal, and changing access rights | the receiver is not authorized for information with a restriction | Authority of `references/planning.md` |

Publishing a knowledge item outside the organization is an act of the knowledge management process.

## Performing information management

Outcomes served: information handled, information available.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Plan | shall produce and deliver documents as the documentation plan of `references/planning.md` sets |
| Receivers | shall publish, distribute or make information available to the designated and authorized stakeholders by agreed lists and schedules or in defined circumstances |
| Delivery | shall distribute a document in the established way to the audience the strategy names, confirming its delivery where needed |
| Distribution record | shall record the receivers and the delivery in the work product distribution register, such as the release page or the list of readers notified |
| Form | should provide information in a form the receiver accepts |
| Official documentation | shall provide official documentation - certificates, evidence of accreditation, licences, assessment ratings - as the requirements set |
