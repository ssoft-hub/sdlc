# Keeping stored information

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| disposing of information, deleting a document or rewriting a stored record | the information that the request asks to dispose of has to be retained | Before disposal; Disposal; Archive; Disposal record |

## Performing information management

Outcomes served: information handled, status recorded.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Obsolete documents | should keep an obsolete document from unintended use, and mark one retained as obsolete |
| Important materials | shall store important materials by the requirements for records, security, maintenance and backup |
| Environment | should store information in an environment that protects it from damage and loss, so that it can be retrieved and read |
| Review | shall review stored information on the period or the event the maintenance actions of `references/planning.md` set: check its state, consider the need to copy or migrate it to another medium, and keep it current, complete and valid |
| Review record | shall record each review of stored information with what it found |
| Archive | shall archive the information designated for it - for audit, the preservation of knowledge and the closure of the project -, the project's results and reports in the environment the contract defines and, on the disposal of the system, the information gathered over its whole life included |
| Archive media | should choose the media, the location and the protection of the archive by the periods of retention and retrieval, the organization's policy, the agreements and the legislation |
| After closure | should retain the needed items after the closure of the project as agreed |
| Before disposal | should evaluate the legal status of the information and the retention period it requires before disposing of it |
| Disposal | shall dispose of unwanted, invalid, corrupted or unverifiable information by the organization's policy and the requirements of security and privacy |
| Disposal record | shall record what was disposed of, when and by what decision |
