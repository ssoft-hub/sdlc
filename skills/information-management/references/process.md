# The information management process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | in the interests of the stakeholders, information is generated, obtained, confirmed, transformed, retained, retrieved and distributed, and unneeded information is disposed of, so that the designated stakeholders receive information that is unambiguous, complete, valid, verifiable, consistent, modifiable, traceable, presentable, relevant, timely and, where required, confidential, during the life cycle and after its end; for software, the recorded information the processes produce is developed and maintained | - |
| information defined | the information to be managed is defined | the strategy defined and the documentation plan recorded, or the project's rules serving as them; the items managed and those retained after the end of the life cycle named |
| representations defined | the forms of representation of information are defined | the content, the formats and the structure of the items, the documentation standards and the templates defined |
| information handled | information is obtained, developed, transformed, stored, checked, presented and disposed of, and is current, complete and valid | each document developed by the standards, examined, approved by the role the strategy names and its approval recorded; each item a change made out of date named or updated; the records of the reviews of stored information; the archive and the records of disposal |
| status recorded | the status of information is recorded | the version, the date, the distribution and the classification of each item, such as in the history of the version control system |
| information available | information is available to the designated stakeholders | each item in the place the strategy names, open to its designated receivers; the distribution records |
