# Work products of information management

"This process" as a source or a receiver is a task of information management passing the work
product to a later one. The stakeholders are those information is provided to, the receivers and the
approvers of a document included; the acquirer is the other party to the agreement; the external
sources are the bodies that publish documentation standards.
