# The portfolio management process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | the necessary, sufficient and suitable projects are initiated and sustained to meet the strategic goals of the organization: the selected projects receive adequate funding and resources and the authority they need, and are assessed continually, to confirm that each justifies further investment or can be redirected to justify it | - |
| opportunities qualified | business opportunities, investments or needs are qualified, prioritized and selected, with regard to risk | the opportunities and potential projects evaluated against the organization's criteria and prioritized, and the decision selecting them recorded |
| projects defined | the projects are defined | each selected project recorded with its scope, its goals, objectives and expected outcomes defined, and the decision authorizing it recorded |
| resources allocated | resources and budgets are allocated to each project | the resources, the budget, the duties and the constraints of each project recorded and committed |
| authority defined | project management responsibilities, accountability and authority are defined | who manages each project, with that party's accountability and authority, the reporting requirements and the milestones, recorded |
| projects sustained | projects meeting the agreement and the stakeholder requirements are sustained | each assessment of a project's viability recorded, with the decision to continue it |
| projects redirected or terminated | projects not meeting the agreement or the stakeholder requirements are redirected or terminated | the decision to redirect, suspend or cancel a project recorded with its grounds |
| projects closed | projects that completed their agreements and satisfied the stakeholder requirements are closed | the decision closing the project recorded, on the record of closure of the agreement or the confirmation that the project is finished |
