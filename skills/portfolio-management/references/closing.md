# Closing a project

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| closing a project; removing a closed project from the portfolio | an item of the agreement on the project's products and services is known not to be met; a product, an activity or a task is not completed | Grounds, Closure |

## Terminating projects

Outcomes served: projects closed.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Grounds | shall take as grounds for closing a project the record of closure of the agreement the acquisition or the supply process passes, or the confirmation of project assessment and control that the project is finished |
| Closure | shall act to close a project after every item of the agreement on its products and services is met, under the agreement and the organization's policy and procedures |
| Documentation | should treat the project documentation that remains after closure as the organization's responsibility, kept where the organization names |
| Removal | may remove a closed project from the portfolio on a decision of the organization |
