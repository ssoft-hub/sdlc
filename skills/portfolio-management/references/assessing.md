# Assessing a project

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| continuing or redirecting a project | - | Viability, Monitoring, Record, Report, Risks, Process departures |
| suspending or cancelling a project | the agreement is known not to allow it; a condition that can be checked before the act but is not confirmed counts as unmet, and the act waits; a condition that can be checked only at acceptance is named and left to the acceptance checks | Disbenefits, Record, Report, Risks, Process departures |

## Assessing the portfolio

Outcomes served: projects sustained, projects redirected or terminated.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Viability | shall collect and analyse data on each project's progress to confirm its continuing viability: it moves towards its goals and objectives, complies with the project directives, is conducted under the life cycle policy, processes and procedures, and remains viable - the need for the service persists, the product is feasible, the benefit from the investment is acceptable |
| Monitoring | shall use the portfolio data to monitor each project against its agreements and the stakeholder requirements |
| Record | shall record each assessment with its grounds as the review record |
| Report | shall report each assessment to the organization as the project status report |
| Risks | shall pass the risks to a project that the assessment finds to the risk management process, for its risk management plan |
| Process departures | shall name each departure from the life cycle policy, processes and procedures that the assessment finds to the life cycle model management process, for the process repository |
| Continuation | shall act, on the recorded assessment, to continue a project that progresses satisfactorily, or to redirect one expected to progress satisfactorily once redirected |
| Shortfall | shall act, on the recorded assessment, to redirect, suspend or cancel a project whose agreement, stakeholder requirements or business benefit will not be achieved |

## Terminating projects

Outcomes served: projects redirected or terminated.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Disbenefits | shall act, where the agreements allow it, to cancel or suspend a project whose disbenefits or risks to the organization outweigh the benefits of continued investment |

For a change to an agreement that such a decision requires, the agent shall pass a change request to
the acquisition or the supply process, which decides it.
