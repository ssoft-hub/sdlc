# A project supplying under an agreement

## Planning project and technical management

Outcomes served: plans defined, roles defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Structure | shall consider the acquirer's requirements in defining the structure of the project's management and of its quality assurance |
| Requirements for the plans | shall include in the requirements for the plans the resources needed and the participation of the acquirer |
| Project management plan | shall base the project management plan on those requirements and on the supply option selected |
| Its content | shall cover in the project management plan the organizational structure of the project and its authority, external organizations included; the engineering environment; the work breakdown structure, with the budget, the performers, the resources, the sizes and the schedules of the work; the management of quality, safety, security and the other critical requirements; the management of subcontractors; quality assurance, verification and validation; the participation of the acquirer and of the users; risk management; the policy of information protection; the official approvals - regulation, certification, rights, licences, warranties; the means of planning, supervision and reporting; and the training of personnel |
