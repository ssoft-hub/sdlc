# Roles and authority in the project

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| assigning roles and authority | - | Roles, Allocation |

Assigning a person to a project or a role is also an act of the human resource management process.

## Planning project and technical management

Outcomes served: roles defined, resources requested.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Roles | shall define and record the roles, responsibility, accountability and authority in the project |
| Persons and groups | shall name the persons and groups involved in the project and affected by it |
| Allocation | shall allocate the tasks and duties among them |
| Commitments | shall check that the commitments are understood, accepted, funded and achievable |
| Scope of the roles | should cover in the roles the project organization, its staffing and the development of the staff's skills |
| Scope of the authority | should cover in the authority the legally responsible positions and persons, for example for the design, safety, certification or accreditation |
| Skills | shall define the project's requirements for experience, knowledge and skills, and apply them in selecting the persons and groups |
