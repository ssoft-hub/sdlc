# The project planning process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | effective and workable plans are produced and coordinated and communicated to the stakeholders; the process defines the scope of the project management and technical activities, the outputs, tasks and deliveries, and schedules with their achievement criteria and resources, and continues throughout the project, with the plans reviewed regularly | - |
| plans defined | goals and plans are defined and recorded | the project's requirements and plans recorded where the project names, the plans holding the scope, the life cycle model, the work breakdown, the schedule, the estimates and the budget, and each plan adopted by a decision |
| roles defined | roles, responsibility, accountability and authority are defined | the roles, the persons and groups assigned to them, and their duties and authority recorded, each assignment made by a decision |
| resources requested | the resources and services needed for the goals are formally requested and transferred | each request for resources and services recorded with the commitment obtained for it |
| plans activated | the plans for performing the project are started and maintained | the execution of an adopted plan directed, the planning data distributed, and each review and change of a plan recorded |
