# A project planned whole

## Defining the project

Outcomes served: plans defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Requirements | shall define and record the project's requirements: its goals, reasons, constraints, boundaries and the work ahead |
| Scope | shall define the project's scope as the agreement establishes it |
| Goals and constraints | should cover in the goals and constraints the performance and the other aspects of quality, the cost, the time and the customer's satisfaction, and the motives of the project |
| Detail of the goals | should define each goal in enough detail to select, tailor and implement the processes and activities |
| Its activities | should include in the scope all the activities needed to meet the business decision criteria and to complete the project, and name in it the life cycle stages the project is responsible for |
| Maintaining the plans | should provide in the planning for the measures by which the plans are maintained while the project is assessed and controlled |

## Planning project and technical management

Outcomes served: plans defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Gate criteria | shall define the achievement criteria for passing each stage's decision gate, delivery dates - in the product release strategy and release plan -, and major interdependencies with external inputs and outputs |
| Costs and budget | shall define the costs and plan the budget |
| Interfaces | shall define and agree the interfaces between the project's elements, and with other projects, organizational units and affected parties |
| Content of the plans | shall cover in them the scale and goals, activities and tasks, deliverable products, schedule, effort estimate, resources, allocation of tasks and duties, quantified risks of the tasks and the process in the risk management plan, quality assurance for the whole project, costs, environment and infrastructure, life cycle model, interfaces and communication mechanisms |
| Internal analyses | should set the intervals between internal analyses by the organization's policy, by the significance for the business, the criticality of the system, and the time and technical risks |
| Costs | should base the costs on the schedule, the effort estimates, and the costs of infrastructure, acquisitions and enabling systems, and include in them a reserve for risks |
| Attributes | should take the quality goals and the risks into account in estimating the project's attributes |
| Their content | should include in the project's attributes the business goals and the quality goals, the size and complexity, the effort, the schedule and the budget |
| Other processes | should define the strategies and tasks of the other processes together with the planning and integrate them into the plans, the risk responses that risk management selects and the plan for implementing an improvement that the life cycle model management process asks for included; the project assessment and control process checks the plans for comprehensiveness, balance and feasibility |

## Activating the project

Outcomes served: plans activated.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Authority | shall obtain authority for the project before its work begins |
| Its source | should take it from portfolio management, whose authorization is the decision opening the project's work |

The agent may activate the plans by a project kick-off meeting.
