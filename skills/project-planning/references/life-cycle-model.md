## Defining the project

Outcomes served: plans defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Life cycle model | shall define and maintain the project's life cycle model, made of stages, on the basis of the organization's models |
| Fit | shall make the project's life cycle and strategy correspond to its volume, context, scale and complexity |
| Source of the models | should take the models applied in the project from those the life cycle model management process gives; the general set of stages is concept, development, production, utilization, support and retirement |
| Processes | shall define and maintain the processes applied in the project |
| Their basis | should base the project's processes on the organization's processes |
| Process definition | should name in each process definition its entry criteria, its inputs, its sequence constraints - predecessor and successor -, its concurrency requirements, its measures of effectiveness, and the parameters of its scope of application and of its cost |
