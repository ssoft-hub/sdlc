# Resources, infrastructure and acquisitions

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| requesting resources and accepting a commitment for them | the request exceeds the project's authority or resources or the scope of the agreement; authority for the project has not been obtained | Requests |

## Planning project and technical management

Outcomes served: plans defined, resources requested.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Resources | shall define the resources for the tasks, and the infrastructure, the environment and the services the project needs, recording the infrastructure the project needs in the infrastructure requirements and its environment in the development environment plan |
| Infrastructure | should name in the definition of the infrastructure the capabilities needed, their suitability and their allocation to tasks - facilities, tools, communications, information technology assets - and the requirements for the enabling systems at each stage |
| Acquisition | shall plan the acquisition of the materials and the services of enabling systems that are supplied from outside the project: the acquisition in the acquisition plan, their delivery and support in the logistics support plan |
| Steps of the acquisition | should provide in the acquisition plan, where needed, for the request for supply, the supplier selection, the acceptance, the administration of the contract and its closure; the acquisition planned is carried out by the agreement processes: the acquisition process, and the supply process where the project supplies under an agreement of its own |
| Content of the acquisition plan | shall include in the acquisition plan the requirements for the system, its planned use, the type of contract, the responsibility of the organizations taking part, the support concept, and the risks considered with the methods of managing them |

## Activating the project

Outcomes served: resources requested.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Requests | shall formally submit the requests for the resources and services needed, and obtain commitments for them, for the resources of the parties responsible for risk management included, recording each request with the commitment obtained for it |
