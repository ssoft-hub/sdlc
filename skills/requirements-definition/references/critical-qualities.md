# Risks, criticality and critical qualities

## Defining system requirements

Outcomes served: system described, requirements defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Critical requirements | shall define the system requirements that relate to risks, to the criticality of the system and to critical quality characteristics, those for security, adaptability and resilience against parties acting against the system included |
| Critical characteristics | should cover health, safety, security, reliability, availability and maintainability |
| Functions | should define the functions that follow from critical quality characteristics, such as diagnostics or backup |
| Safety | should consider the aspects of safety - the methods of operation and support, the environmental impact, injury to personnel - and each safety-related function with its integrity in terms of the risk reduction required |
| Security | should consider the aspects of security, trade-offs and the protection of critical information and data included, and identify the security risks under the applicable standards: administrative, personnel, physical, computer, communications, network, emanations, environment |
