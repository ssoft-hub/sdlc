# Critical performance measures

## Analysing system requirements

Outcomes served: measures defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Critical performance measures | shall define and record the critical performance measures by which the degree of technical achievement is assessed |
| Their content | should define for each measure of effectiveness in the system requirements the technical measures, the quality measures and the critical performance parameters, and analyse and examine the critical measures to show that the requirements are met and to identify the risks to cost, dates and operation from nonconformance |
