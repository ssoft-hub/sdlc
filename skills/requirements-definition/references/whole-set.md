# Requirements specified, baselined and kept whole

## Defining system requirements

Outcomes served: requirements defined, traceability established.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Intended use | shall analyse the intended use of the system |
| Specification | shall have the requirements specification describe the functions and capabilities of the system; the business, organizational and user requirements; the safety, security, ergonomics, interface, operation and maintenance requirements; the design constraints and the qualification requirements |
| All requirements | should define, iteratively and recursively with the other processes down the system hierarchy, all the system requirements related to the stakeholder requirements, the functional boundaries, the functions, the constraints, the cost targets, the interactions and the critical quality characteristics, and record them in a form fit for management throughout the life cycle, with their rationale, decisions and assumptions |
| Product and service requirements | shall state the system requirements as the product or service requirements where the system is acquired or supplied under an agreement |
| Change rationale | should give a change request for a requirement the rationale that shows its acceptability and its conformance to the stakeholder requirements |

## Managing system requirements

Outcomes served: requirements defined, traceability established.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Distribution | shall establish a means by which the requirements and their updates reach every party that works to them |
| Configuration | should maintain the requirements - defining, recording and managing their baseline - under configuration management, with the changes that come from architecture definition, design definition and other processes |
| Baseline items | shall maintain the key information items selected for baselines |
| Candidates | should propose the system requirements as the candidates for the baseline, which the configuration management process establishes and maintains |
| Traceability over the life cycle | should keep the traceability in both directions throughout the life cycle, in the place the project keeps the requirements - with the stakeholder requirements, the architecture elements, the interface definitions, the analysis results, the verification methods and the allocated and derived requirements -, so that each achievable stakeholder requirement is met by at least one system requirement and each system requirement serves at least one stakeholder requirement |
