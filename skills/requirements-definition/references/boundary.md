# The boundary, the operational environment and the description of functions

## Preparing for system requirements definition

Outcomes served: system described.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Functional boundary | shall define and record the functional boundary of the system - the behaviour and properties it is to provide - on the basis of the stakeholder requirements |
| Its basis | should build it from the context of use and the operational scenarios of stakeholder needs and requirements definition, describe the stimuli of the system, how it satisfies the users' needs, its behaviour in its environment and its interactions with the operational environment - interaction properties and constraints such as flows of data and procedures -, and state the expected behaviour at the boundary quantitatively |

## Defining system requirements

Outcomes served: requirements defined.

The agent may state the conditions of a function by the states and modes of the system, and
describe the requirements by several modelling methods and views.

## Analysing system requirements

Outcomes served: system described, requirements analysed.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Operational environment | shall understand the impact of the system and software requirements on the operational environment: identify the interfaces between the requirements and its other elements, and the influence the requirements have on it |
| Its changes | shall identify the changes of the operational environment |
| What it holds | should count in it the tasks the intended users perform and the other systems they use |
