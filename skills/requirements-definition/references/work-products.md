# Work products of system/software requirements definition

The outcomes are those `references/process.md` names. "This process" as a source is the work product of the
system level, which the same process takes at the level of the software elements. The acquirer is
the other party to the agreement, from which the customer's requests and change requests come; the
stakeholders are the individuals and organizations with an interest in the system, to whom the
analysed requirements are fed back and who agree them; neither is a process.
