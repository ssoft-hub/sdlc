## Preparing for system requirements definition

Outcomes served: requirements defined, enabling inputs defined, enabling systems available.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Strategy | shall develop the system requirements definition strategy |
| Its decision | should present a strategy it has defined with the output of the work for a decision |
| Its approach | should name in it the approach to defining the requirements and to managing them throughout the life cycle |
| Enabling systems | shall identify and plan the enabling systems and services the definition needs, tools for requirements included, and define the inputs for the requirements for them |
| Access | shall obtain or acquire access to them |
| Their requirements | should define the requirements for them and the interactions with them |
| Their fitness | should have the validation process confirm objectively that each achieves its intended use |
