# Incidents and problems

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| a response to a problem other than its elimination: accepting it as is, deferring it, closing it without resolution | the problem resolution has not been checked; an evaluation has found a nonconformity to a criterion; an evaluation that the strategy requires has not been performed or has been performed without the required independence | Analysis; Options |
| an alert notification to parties outside the project | the receiver is not authorized for information with a restriction; a condition that can be checked before the act but is not confirmed counts as unmet; a condition that can be checked only at acceptance is named and left to the acceptance checks | Alert |

## Preparing for quality assurance

Outcomes served: procedures implemented.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Closed loop | shall establish a problem resolution process for all problems, nonconformities included, detected in the products and the activities, as a closed loop: each problem detected is reported and entered into the process, the actions on it are defined, the parties concerned are informed, its causes are identified and eliminated where possible, its resolution is reached, its status is tracked and reported, and the problem reports are maintained as the agreement provides |
| Categories and priorities | should include in the process a scheme of categories and priorities that classifies each problem by category and priority for trend analysis and resolution |
| Change requests | may apply or adapt the loop to handling, tracking and controlling change requests for software, which the configuration management process identifies and records |

## Responding to incidents and problems

Outcomes served: evaluations performed, results provided.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Incident | shall record each incident, identify it uniquely, analyse it, classify it by severity, and give initial support and feedback on it |
| Problem reported | shall report each problem detected - by evaluations, verification, validation, operation, maintenance, the users -, a nonconformity included, at once, enter it into the problem resolution process and draw up a report describing it |
| Outside the work | shall report and record a problem it finds outside the assigned work as well, and leave its correction to a decision |
| Problem recorded | shall record each problem, identify it uniquely, classify it by severity, and give initial support and feedback on it |
| Response due | should give each problem a response |
| Other attributes | may classify a problem also by other attributes, such as criticality, urgency and relevance |
| Incident resolved or raised | shall resolve an incident or raise it to a problem |
| Urgent correction | shall give a problem that needs resolution before the actual change an urgent correction |
| Analysis | shall analyse and classify each problem - investigate and diagnose its cause, evaluate its impact, determine and agree the actions and the resolution -, the deviations from the agreed requirements and from the organization's quality goals included |
| Options | should name the possible response options in the results of the analysis |
| Known error | should consider in the analysis whether the problem is a known error and whether it affects applications on several platforms |
| Response | shall set the priority of the responses to problems and carry them out: carry out the actions that eliminate a problem and check their implementation by a review, and submit a change request for a diagnosed error |
| Corrective action register | shall record each corrective action and its state in the corrective action register, such as the record of the problem |
| Alert | shall accompany, where needed, a problem of high severity that affects other systems or users with an alert notification until the correction or the change |
| Trends | shall identify and analyse the trends in incidents and problems by their categories and priorities |
| Improvement opportunity | shall pass each improvement the trends show to life cycle model management as an improvement opportunity |
| Status | shall keep the stakeholders informed of the status of incidents and problems, and the current status of each recorded problem known, such as by a problem status report |
| Tracking | shall track incidents and problems to their resolution and closure |
| Lessons | should record the lessons learned from incidents and problems and take them into account in the work of the project |
| Closure | shall close a problem as resolved only once the evaluation of its resolution shows the problem resolved, the adverse trend eliminated, the change correctly implemented in the products and the activities, and no new problem created |

The change a response calls for is left to the technical processes of what changes, and a response
that changes the plan, the resources or the schedule to project assessment and control. Closing a
problem without resolution is an act of the table of this file, which the step Closure does not
govern.
