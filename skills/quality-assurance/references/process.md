# The quality assurance process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | the organization's quality management is applied effectively to the project, giving confidence that the quality requirements will be met: the products, services and processes of the project are evaluated proactively for conformance to their conditions, plans, requirements and agreements, by independent audit included, and each problem found is identified, analysed, controlled and resolved | - |
| procedures implemented | the quality assurance procedures of the project are defined and implemented | the strategy defined and the plan recorded, or the project's rules serving as them; the form of the quality record, the audit strategy and the problem resolution process defined |
| criteria defined | the quality assurance evaluation criteria and methods are defined | for each evaluation, its criteria and methods named before it is performed |
| evaluations performed | evaluations of the products, services and processes of the project are performed, consistent with the quality policy, procedures and requirements | the quality record of each evaluation; each audit report and the record of what its parties agreed; each problem recorded, analysed and tracked to its closure; each corrective action in the corrective action register |
| results provided | the results of the evaluations are provided to the relevant stakeholders | the results passed to the owners of what was evaluated and to quality management; the status of incidents and problems known to the stakeholders, such as in a problem status report |
