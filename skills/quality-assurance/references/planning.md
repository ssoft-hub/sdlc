## Preparing for quality assurance

Outcomes served: procedures implemented, criteria defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Strategy | shall define the quality assurance strategy of the project, taking it from the project's rules - review requirements, automated checks, readiness criteria - and naming what is missing, and establish and carry out a quality assurance process fitting the conditions of the project, whose aim is that its products and processes conform to the established requirements and plans |
| Content of the strategy | should make the strategy consistent with the organization's quality management policy, procedures and strategy, and include in it the roles, the responsibility, the accountability and the authority; the activities for each life cycle process and for each supplier, subcontractors included; the verification, validation, monitoring, measurement, inspection and testing the product requires; the acceptance criteria of the product or the service and the criteria of the evaluations |
| Quality management | should define in the strategy its relationship with the organization's quality management, so that the organization's quality policy and procedures are followed |
| Plan | shall develop, record, carry out and maintain for the duration of the agreement a quality assurance plan: the quality standards, methods, procedures and means; the procedures for reviewing the agreement; the procedures for identifying, collecting, recording, maintaining and distributing quality records; the resources, the schedule and the responsibility; the verification, validation, review, audit and problem resolution activities selected |
| Coordination | should coordinate quality assurance with verification, validation, review and audit, defining their tasks and monitoring their performance |
| Record form | shall define a form of quality record that shows the conformance of the processes and the work products to their quality requirements |
| Independence | shall establish the independence of quality assurance: those responsible for conformance to the requirements have the organizational freedom, the resources and the authority to resolve problems and to check their resolution |

## Quality assurance records and reports

Outcomes served: results provided.
