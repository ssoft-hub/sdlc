# Audits

The problems an audit finds also take the rules of `references/problems.md`. The auditing party is
the project where it audits its own work or a supplier's; the audited party is the project where
the acquirer or another party audits it.

## Preparing for quality assurance

Outcomes served: procedures implemented, criteria defined.

| Subject | Party | What the agent does |
|---|---|---|
| Audit strategy | auditing | shall define and carry out an audit strategy: its purpose, scope, phases, audit criteria and audit team |
| Milestones | auditing | shall conduct the audits at the milestones the project plans specify |

## Product or service evaluations

Outcomes served: evaluations performed, results provided.

| Step | Party | What the agent does |
|---|---|---|
| Agreement on the audit | both | shall have the participating parties agree on the audit resources and, for each audit, on the agenda, the products and results audited, the scope and the procedures, and the entry and exit criteria |
| Auditors | auditing | shall have the audit conducted by independent, impartial and objective auditors with no direct responsibility for the products and the activities audited |
| Conformance | auditing | shall determine, by the audit of the selected products, services or processes under the audit strategy, their conformance to the requirements, the plans and the agreements, and record the nonconformities |
| Software audited | auditing | shall confirm by the audit of software that the code reflects the design documentation; the acceptance conditions and the testing requirements are adequate for acceptance; the test data conform to the specifications; the products are successfully tested and satisfy the specifications; the test reports are correct and the discrepancies between actual and expected results eliminated; the user documentation conforms to the standards; the activities are carried out under the approved requirements, plans and agreement; the costs and the schedules are consistent with the plans |
| Report | auditing | shall record the results in an audit report and distribute and present it to the audited party |
| Answer | audited | shall confirm the problems found and report the resolutions it plans |
| Result agreed | both | shall have the parties agree on the result of the audit, the obligations on the action items and the criteria for closing them |
| Communication record | both | shall record what the parties agreed and make the record available to the audited party |
| Problems | both | shall record the problems the audit finds, communicate them to those responsible for corrective action and pass them to problem resolution, where the person assigned takes the corrective action - at once or after root cause analysis - and it is tracked to resolution |
| Review by the auditor | auditing | may review the nonconforming resolutions and their results |
