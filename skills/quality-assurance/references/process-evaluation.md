# Evaluating the processes of the project

The processes of a supplier take the rules of `references/agreement.md`.

## Process evaluations

Outcomes served: evaluations performed.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Life cycle processes | shall evaluate the life cycle processes of the project - supply, development, operation, maintenance and support, quality assurance itself included - for conformance: that they meet the agreement and the standards and are carried out under the plans |
| Tools and environment | shall evaluate the means and the environment that support or automate the processes - the development practices, the development and test environment, the reused components, the automated build - for conformance to the agreement and the requirements |
| Product and measurement | shall ensure that the software product and the measurement process conform to the established standards and procedures |
| Personnel | shall ensure that the assigned personnel have the skills and the knowledge the project requires and receive the training needed, which the human resource management process provides |
| Quality management | may carry out additional quality management activities under ISO 9001 |
