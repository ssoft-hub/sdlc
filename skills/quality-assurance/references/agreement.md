# Under an agreement

## Product or service evaluations

Outcomes served: evaluations performed.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Plans | shall ensure that all the plans the agreement requires are recorded, conform to it, are consistent with each other and are carried out |
| Delivery | shall check, in preparing for delivery, that the product fully satisfies the requirements of the agreement and is acceptable to the acquirer |

## Process evaluations

Outcomes served: evaluations performed.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Supplier's processes | shall evaluate the supplier's processes for conformance to the process requirements, pass the requirements of the prime agreement to the subcontractor, and ensure that its products satisfy them |
| Supplier's context | should take into account in that evaluation the joint development environment, the process measures the supplier is to provide and the process risks it is obliged to take into account |
| Support | shall ensure that the acquirer and the other parties receive the support and the cooperation the agreement and the plans provide for |

## Quality assurance records and reports

Outcomes served: results provided.

The agent shall make the records of quality assurance available to the acquirer as the agreement
defines.
