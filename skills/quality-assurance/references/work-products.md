# Work products of quality assurance

"This process" as a source or a receiver is a task of quality assurance passing the work product to
a later one. The stakeholders are those informed of the results of the evaluations and of the status
of incidents and problems, within the project and outside it; the audited party is whoever owns the
products or the processes an audit checks, a project process or a supplier; those responsible for
resolving a problem are those a corrective action is assigned to, in any technical process; the
external sources are the bodies that publish standards.
