# Work products of quality management

The outcomes are those `process.md` names. Where this process is both the source and the receiver,
the work product is the version in force that the process updates, or passes from one task of
quality management to a later one. The acquirer and the users of the product, and the other
organizations and the industry that supply benchmarking data, are parties outside the life
cycle; organization management is a party within the organization, not a process. "All life
cycle processes" means every process of the life cycle takes the work product: each is performed
under the organization's policy and procedures.
