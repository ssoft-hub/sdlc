# Quality assurance results and reviews

## Assessing quality management

Outcomes served: results analysed, policy improved.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Quality assurance results | shall gather the results of the quality assurance evaluations, analyse them against the quality evaluation criteria and record the analysis as the analysis report |
| Management review | shall present regularly to the top management - the owner or the maintainers who set the rules on quality - the achievement of the quality goals against these criteria for its review, and record each review as the review record |
| Customer satisfaction | shall assess customer satisfaction: gather data on it, to check the continual improvement of quality at the level of the organization and of the project, and draw up a report |
| Project quality assurance | shall analyse periodically, by a review plan it records, the projects' quality assurance activities and plans for consistency with the organization's quality goals, policy and procedures |
| Improvements | shall monitor the status of improvements to the quality of processes, products and services |
| Actual management | shall check the actual quality management against the quality goals |
| Review of the policy | shall review the quality policy and procedures on the basis of the results of the projects and the organization these steps gather |
| Improvement | shall propose each improvement the review finds as a change to a rule on quality, an act of the table of `SKILL.md` |
