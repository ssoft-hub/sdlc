## Planning quality management

Outcomes served: quality policy implemented, resources provided.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Policy | shall establish and record the organization's quality policy, standards and procedures, and its ultimate and current quality goals |
| Procedures | shall define and record the quality procedures at the level of the organization, with the resources and duties they need, and pass them to the life cycle model management process for its process repository |
| Duties | shall define and record the duties and authority for implementing quality management, for example who establishes and changes the rules on quality, who evaluates, and who decides on a corrective action |
| Resources | shall provide quality management and the projects with resources - people, time, means - and information, the projects for performing and monitoring quality assurance activities |
| Quality plan | shall record in the quality plan the information given to the projects - the policy, goals, criteria and methods of quality - and pass it to the quality assurance process |
