# The quality management process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | products, services and implementations of the quality management process meet the quality goals of the organization and of the project, and achieve customer satisfaction | - |
| quality policy implemented | the organization's quality goals, policy and procedures are defined and implemented | the quality policy, goals and procedures, with the duties and authority for quality, recorded where the project names, and the project's processes performed under them |
| evaluation criteria established | quality evaluation criteria and methods are established | the standards, references and measures by which the achievement of each quality goal is measured and checked, recorded |
| resources provided | resources and information are provided to the projects to support the operation and monitoring of quality assurance activities | the people, time and means for quality assurance assigned, and the policy, goals, criteria and methods available where they are applied |
| results analysed | quality assurance evaluation results are gathered and analysed | the analysis of the quality assurance results against the criteria, and the report on customer satisfaction |
| policy improved | the quality policy and procedures are improved on the basis of project and organizational results | each improvement to the policy or the procedures put into effect by a decision, and each corrective or preventive action recorded with its result |
