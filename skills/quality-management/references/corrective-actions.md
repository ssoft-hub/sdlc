## Corrective and preventive actions

Outcomes served: policy improved.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Cause | should establish, before a corrective or preventive action is planned, the cause of the failure to achieve a quality goal, or of the risk of it |
| Need | should evaluate the need for an action |
| Corrective action | shall plan a corrective action when a quality goal is not achieved, at the level of the project as well as of the organization |
| Preventive action | shall plan a preventive action when there is a risk that a quality goal will not be achieved |
| Content of a corrective action | may include in a corrective action the correction of a product created in the project, a change to the planned actions, or both |
| Content of a preventive action | may include in a preventive action a change to the product specification, to the process definition, or both, so that the failure does not recur |
| Carrying out | shall carry out a corrective action adopted by a decision at the level of the organization |
| Within a project | shall pass a corrective action within a project to the project assessment and control process, which carries it out, and follow it to completion |
| Communication | shall communicate the action and its results to the relevant stakeholders, within the organization included |
| Proportion | should keep a corrective or preventive action proportionate to the consequences of the nonconformity or of its risk |
| Result | should record the result of the action in the corrective action register once it is carried out |
| Effectiveness | should check the effectiveness of the action against the cause it was to eliminate |

The agent shall plan the action also where the cause is not found. Managing the change by which a
product is corrected is left to the configuration management process, and making it to the technical
processes. For a change of a process definition, the agent shall pass a change request to the life
cycle model management process, which decides it.
