# A work product other than code verified

## Performing verification

Outcomes served: element verified.

| Object | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Requirements | shall verify that the system requirements are consistent, feasible and testable and are allocated to the hardware and software elements and the manual operations by the design criteria, that the software requirements are consistent, feasible and verifiable and reflect the system requirements accurately, and that the safety, security and criticality requirements are correct, as rigorous methods show |
| Design | shall verify that the design is correct, consistent with the requirements and traceable to them, provides the proper sequence of events, inputs, outputs, interfaces, logic, allocation of timing and sizing, and error detection, isolation and recovery, is derived from the requirements, and implements the safety, security and other critical requirements correctly, as rigorous methods show |
| Integration | shall verify that the components and units of each software element are completely and correctly integrated into it, that the software elements, the hardware and the manual operations are integrated into the system, and that the integration tasks are performed under the integration plan |
| Documentation | shall verify that the documentation is adequate, complete and consistent, is prepared in a timely manner, and that the management of its configuration follows the established procedures |
