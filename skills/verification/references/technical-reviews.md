# Technical reviews

A technical review is an evaluation of the project's software products or services by the
participating parties against an agreed agenda and criteria, a kind of inspection. Management reviews
belong to the project assessment and control process, joint reviews with the other party to an
agreement to the acquisition and supply processes.

## Acts of this situation

Accepting a product with a nonconformity on the results of a technical review, conditionally
included, is an act of the acts table of `references/problems.md`.

## Preparing for verification

Outcomes served: element verified, enabling systems available.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Dates | shall hold the technical reviews of the project's own products at the dates the project plans set in advance, with the scope and participants the project needs |
| Ad hoc reviews | should have the stakeholders determine the need for ad hoc technical reviews beyond the planned ones |
| Other parties | may admit other parties to an ad hoc review by agreement |
| Resources | shall provide all the resources a technical review needs: personnel, a location, service facilities, hardware and software, tools |

## Performing verification

Outcomes served: element verified, evidence provided.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Agenda | shall have the participants of each technical review agree on its agenda, the products and problems considered, its scope and procedures, and its entry and exit criteria |
| Material | shall collect, prepare and distribute the review material |
| Review | shall hold the technical review - evaluate the products and services and present evidence that they are complete, conform to the accepted standards and specifications, that their changes are properly implemented and affect only the areas configuration management identified, that they adhere to the established schedules, are ready for the next planned work, and are developed, operated or maintained under the project's plans, schedules, standards and guidelines - and record its results as planned |

## Managing verification results

Outcomes served: report made.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Analysis | shall analyse the technical review report - identify and record the risks and the problems, propose solutions on the results, determine the priority of the actions - and pass the problems, where required, to problem resolution by the step "Problems" of `references/problems.md` |
| Outcome | shall record the results of the review with the evaluation of its adequacy - acceptance, non-acceptance, or conditional acceptance -, distribute them, and make them available to all the participating and interested parties |
| Agreed outcome | shall have the participants agree on the outcome of the review, on the responsibility for the action items and on the criteria for closing them |
| Actions | shall track the actions on the problems found to closure, and report and record the changes of the work products and processes |
