# Work products of verification

The outcomes are those `references/process.md` names. The acquirer and the other stakeholders are
parties outside the life cycle; the participating parties of a review are those that agree on a
technical review and on its outcome. The contract, the risk management plan, the risk mitigation
plan, the progress status record, the customer request, the acceptance record and the contract
review record are left to the processes that hold the management and joint reviews.
