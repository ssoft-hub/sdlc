## Preparing for verification

Outcomes served: constraints identified, enabling inputs identified, enabling systems available,
element verified, report made.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Balance | should make the verification strategy confirm that the element is built right at the least cost, time and risk |
| Scope | shall define the scope and the actions of verification: the software products that require verification, and the goals of the actions by their scope, size, complexity and criticality |
| Its content | should include in the scope the requirements, architectural characteristics and design properties verified - safety in the planned operational environment and the important quality characteristics among them -, and name for each action its object - the actual system, or a model, mock-up or prototype - and its expected results, so that verification at each stage gives evidence that the stage's outputs satisfy all their requirements |
| Criticality | shall analyse the project requirements for criticality |
| Its measure | may evaluate criticality by the potential of an undetected error to lead to death or injury, mission failure, financial loss or loss of equipment, by the maturity of the technology and the risks of its use, and by the availability of means and resources |
| Verification process | shall establish, where the project provides for verification work, a verification process for the software product |
| Independence | shall define, where that work needs a degree of organizational independence, the conditions for performing it with that degree |
| Independent verification | shall select, where the project provides for independent verification, a qualified organization to perform it, and ensure its independence and authority |
| Constraints on the actions | shall identify the constraints that can limit the feasibility of the actions |
| Their kinds | should consider among them technical feasibility, cost, time, the availability of verification means and qualified personnel, and the contractual constraints, mission criticality included |
| Method kind | should select the method - inspection, analysis, demonstration or testing - by the type of system, the goals of the project and the acceptable risks |
| Agreement on the methods | should agree the methods selected with the stakeholders, so that the approach is acceptable to them |
| Strategy | shall define the verification strategy and implement it: the actions with their methods, techniques and tools, the work products or processes verified, the degree of independence, the schedule |
| What is missing | should propose what a recorded strategy lacks, and present it with the output of the work for a decision |
| Trade-off | should make the strategy a trade-off between scope, constraints and way of performing, ordered by priorities, covering each action's methods and the enabling systems it needs - training simulators, test benches, competent personnel, a location, services |
| Removal | should evaluate by its risks an action considered for removal, an act of the table of `SKILL.md` |
| Updates | should update the strategy and the schedule as the project proceeds, and revise or reschedule the planned actions on unexpected events or a change of the system |
| Plan | shall develop and record a verification plan: the life cycle activities and the products verified, the verification tasks for each activity and product, the resources, the responsibilities, the schedule, and the procedure for forwarding reports to the acquirer and the other stakeholders |
| Regression strategy | shall develop a regression strategy: the checks of the integrated software repeated when its software items change |
| Constraints on the system | shall identify and record the constraints the strategy puts on the system that are to enter the system requirements, the architecture or the design, and pass them to the system/software requirements definition, architecture definition and design definition processes |
| Their content | should relate them to accuracy, uncertainty, repeatability, verification means, measurement methods, the needs of system integration, suitability, availability and the interfaces with the enablers |
| Enabling systems | shall identify and plan the enabling systems and services of verification, and identify the inputs for their requirements for the infrastructure management process |
| Their kinds | should count among them equipment, simulators, test tools and environments |
| Access | shall obtain or acquire access to the enabling systems and services to be used |
| Ways | may lease, buy, develop, reuse or subcontract an enabling system, or obtain it by a combination of these |
| Validation | should have the validation process confirm objectively that an enabling system achieves its intended use |
