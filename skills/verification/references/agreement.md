# Verification under an agreement

## Preparing for verification

Outcomes served: element verified.

The agent shall verify an element acquired from a supplier only as an element of the system,
against the requirements assigned to it, leaving the progress and quality of its development at the
supplier to the supplier monitoring and the acceptance of the acquisition process.

## Performing verification

Outcomes served: element verified, evidence provided.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Acquirer's verification | should verify the acquired products as the section above sets, where the acquirer supplements the supplier monitoring with verification as necessary |
| Supplier's verification | should show by verification, where the project is a supplier under an agreement, that its software products or services and its processes fully satisfy the established requirements |
