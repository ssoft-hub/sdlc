# Choosing an option or a candidate

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| the choice of an acquisition option | a requirement the product does not meet; an acceptance criterion not passed; a licence or terms that do not allow the project's use; missing support; an open item | Options, Recommendation; Named option and Unconfirmed condition of `SKILL.md`; Strategy of `references/procurement.md` |
| the choice of a supplier | a requirement the product does not meet; an acceptance criterion not passed; a licence or terms that do not allow the project's use; missing support; an open item | the criteria rows Source, Evaluation and Agreement; the selection rows Evaluation and Grounds; Off-the-shelf check, Unconfirmed condition, Licence obligations and Meeting them of `SKILL.md` |

## Preparing for the acquisition

Outcomes served: request prepared, supplier selected.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Options | shall compare, where the acquisition option is not yet chosen, the options for meeting the requirements - buying an off-the-shelf product, development within the acquiring organization, development under contract, a combination of these, enhancement of a product already in use - by risk, cost and benefit |
| Recommendation | shall recommend one option and leave the choice to the user |

### The supplier selection criteria

The rows below bind as well the criteria the table of terms of `SKILL.md` maps for an
off-the-shelf product.

| Subject | What the agent does with the criteria |
|---|---|
| Source | shall establish them, before candidates are evaluated, from the organization's business goals and the acquisition strategy, under the organization's supplier selection policy where one exists |
| Evaluation | shall give each criterion the way it is evaluated |
| Compliance | should include in them the compliance requirements a candidate is to meet |
| Agreement | shall have them agreed before it evaluates the candidates: criteria the user's request or the project's rules state are agreed, criteria the agent derives once the user accepts them |

## Selecting the supplier

Outcomes served: supplier selected.

For an off-the-shelf product, the candidates are the available products, each with its
documentation, its licence and its support terms.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Evaluation | shall evaluate each candidate - its stated or assumed capabilities and, where it answered a request for supply, its proposal and its process capabilities - against the requirements and the supplier selection criteria, in line with the acquisition strategy |
| Ranking | should rank competing candidates by that evaluation |
| Recommendation | shall recommend one or more candidates and leave the choice to the user |
| Grounds | should name the grounds of the evaluation for the recommended candidate and for each rejected one |
| Register | shall record the candidate chosen in the preferred suppliers register, for later selection: the list of approved off-the-shelf products or suppliers the project keeps, or, where it keeps none, the acceptance record with the grounds of the choice |
