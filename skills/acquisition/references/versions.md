# A new version, new terms, removal

## Taking a product into the project

Outcomes served: product accepted, obligations satisfied.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| New version | shall treat a new version of an accepted product as a delivery; an off-the-shelf one passes the off-the-shelf check again, its licence and terms included |
| Changed terms | shall treat a changed licence or changed terms of service as a change to the agreement, by the rows "Impact" and "Affected documents" of `references/procurement.md` |

## Payment and closure

Outcomes served: obligations satisfied.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Removal | shall close the licence once the product has left the project and nothing it was built into is still distributed |
