# Accepting a delivery

## Accepting the product or the service

Outcomes served: product accepted.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Supplier's part | should determine how far the supplier takes part in acceptance |
| Preparation | should prepare the test cases and data, the test procedures and the test conditions of acceptance consistent with the acceptance criteria and the acceptance strategy of `references/procurement.md` |
| Checks | shall check a delivery, before it is used or built into the product, against each acceptance criterion by acceptance inspection and testing |
| Failed check | shall record each failed check with what it found |
| Nonconformity | shall name each nonconformity by the term of the agreement or the requirement it breaks |
| Resolution | shall resolve each nonconformity by the procedure the agreement sets |
| Conformity | shall confirm that what is delivered conforms to the terms of the agreement |
| Acceptance | shall accept what is delivered, once the decision that opens the act is taken, from the supplier or from another party the agreement names |
| Notice | shall tell the supplier of the acceptance where the supplier expects it |
