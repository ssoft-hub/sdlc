# The acquisition process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | a product or a service is obtained in accordance with the acquirer's requirements, from the need identified to the product or the service accepted | - |
| request prepared | a request for supply is prepared | the requirements and the acceptance criteria recorded, and, for a supplier that answers the request, the supplier selection criteria |
| supplier selected | one or more suppliers are selected | the decision recorded with the grounds of the evaluation of each candidate; for a product an explicit request names, the acceptance record |
| agreement established | an agreement between the acquirer and the supplier is established | the agreement accepted by both parties: a contract, a licence and terms of service, or a record of an oral understanding |
| product accepted | a product or a service complying with the agreement is accepted | the acceptance record |
| obligations satisfied | the acquirer's obligations under the agreement are satisfied | the payment or the other agreed action done and the record of closure with no open item left, or, for a licence in use, its conditions met |
