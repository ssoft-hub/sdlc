# Procurement

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| sending the request for supply | a requirement the product does not meet; an acceptance criterion not passed; a licence or terms that do not allow the project's use; missing support; an open item | Request, Sending; Unconfirmed condition of `SKILL.md` |
| approval of the requirements developed by the supplier | a requirement the product does not meet; an open item | Supplier's requirements; Check and Unconfirmed condition of `SKILL.md` |
| payment and closure | an open item | Closure, Open items, Notice of completion, Portfolio; Unconfirmed condition of `SKILL.md` |

## Who performs the acquisition

Where the project, itself a supplier under another agreement, gives part of its work to a
subcontractor, the agent should acquire that part from the subcontractor by this skill. The supply
process carries the requirements the prime agreement sets for that part into the agreement with the
subcontractor.

The project may have the activities and tasks of acquisition performed by one or more
organizations it selects to perform the acquisition for it. The agent should inform such an
organization of the acquisition requirements.

## Preparing for the acquisition

Outcomes served: request prepared, supplier selected.

### The acquisition strategy

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Strategy | shall define and record the acquisition strategy for the chosen option, with each item the table below marks shall |
| Further items | should settle in the acquisition strategy each item the table below marks should |

| The acquisition strategy carries | Force |
|---|---|
| the sourcing strategy: where the product or the service comes from | shall |
| the acquisition life cycle model, described or referred to | should |
| the provision for reducing risks and problems | should |
| the milestone dates | should |
| a schedule and a budget | should |
| the supplier selection criteria of `references/choosing.md`, where the supplier is external | should |

### The requirements and the request for supply

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Analysis | may analyse the software requirements within the project or entrust the analysis to a supplier |
| Supplier's requirements | shall not treat requirements a supplier developed as agreed until the acquirer has approved them |
| Readings | shall check each requirement for a reading a supplier could take differently from the one meant |
| Acceptance strategy | shall define and record how and by whom each acceptance criterion is checked |
| Request | shall prepare a request for supply that carries each item the table below marks shall |
| Further items | should add to a request sent to suppliers each item the table below marks should |
| Tailoring | should determine which life cycle processes apply in the acquisition and record the requirements for tailoring them |
| Other parties | should name the processes that parties other than the supplier perform, so that the supplier states in its proposal how it will support their work |
| Scope | shall establish the scope of the tasks the contract defines and carry it into the request and into the agreement |
| Milestones | shall name in the acquisition documentation the milestones at which the supplier's activities are reviewed and checked as part of monitoring |

| The request for supply carries | Force |
|---|---|
| the requirements | shall |
| the acceptance criteria | shall |
| the supplier selection criteria | should |
| the scope | should |
| the instructions to bidders | should |
| the list of software products | should |
| the terms and conditions, the business practice the supplier is expected to agree with included | should |
| the control of subcontractors | should |
| the technical constraints, such as the target environment | should |

## Advertising the acquisition and selecting the supplier

Outcomes served: request prepared, supplier selected.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Sending | shall communicate the request for supply to the potential suppliers the project has identified |
| Need | shall communicate the need for the acquisition to the stakeholders through the channels the project defines |
| Partnership | may accompany the request with a partnership of managers in the supply chain who exchange information with suppliers and acquirers for an agreed or joint approach to common technical and commercial problems |
| Selection | shall select the supplier by the rules of `references/choosing.md` |
| Notice | should tell each supplier that submitted a proposal why it was or was not selected |

## Establishing and maintaining the agreement

Outcomes served: agreement established.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Content | shall check, before the project agrees, that the agreement, whichever party drafted it, carries each item the table below marks shall |
| Further terms | should settle in the agreement each item the table below marks should |
| Negotiation | shall negotiate the agreement with the supplier |
| Established | shall treat the agreement as established once both parties have accepted it |
| Oral understanding | should record an oral understanding |
| Parts | may provide in the agreement for delivering the product or the service, and paying for it, in parts |
| Tailoring parties | may involve other parties - potential suppliers, third parties such as regulatory bodies - in defining the requirements for tailoring the processes, before the contract is concluded |
| Tailoring requirements | shall define the requirements for tailoring the processes with regard to the processes the supplier has adopted |
| In the contract | shall have the contract include the requirements for tailoring the processes or refer to them |

| The agreement carries | Force |
|---|---|
| the requirements on the product or the service | shall |
| the cost, and the schedule with its milestones | shall |
| the acceptance criteria | shall |
| the expectations and duties of both parties | shall |
| the proprietary, usage, ownership, warranty and licensing rights of the reused off-the-shelf products | shall |
| the conditions of verification, validation and acceptance | should |
| the procedures for handling exceptions and for change control | should |
| the payment schedule | should |
| the rights and restrictions on technical data and intellectual property | should |
| the acquisition logistics - support, spare parts, scheduled servicing -, where the maintenance of the product requires it | should |

### A change to the agreement

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Own need | shall name a change the acquirer needs - a new or changed requirement, the redirection, suspension or cancellation of the project - and propose it to the supplier as a change to the agreement |
| Supplier's change | shall consider a change the supplier proposes |
| Impact | shall evaluate, before the project agrees to a change, its impact on the cost, the dates, the quality, the benefit, the duties of both parties and the project's plans, stating "none" for each the change leaves as it is |
| Made | shall not treat a change as made until both parties have agreed it and the agreement records it |
| Record | shall treat a message as the agreement's record of a change only where the agreement is informal and the supplier confirmed the change in that message |
| Affected documents | shall name, once a change is agreed, each document and plan it affects - the requirements, the acceptance criteria, the schedule, the acceptance plan - and whether each needs a correction |
| Mechanism | should define, in a mechanism for managing changes to the agreement it proposes, the roles and responsibilities of management, the formality of change requests and of renegotiation, and the links with the stakeholders |

## Monitoring the agreement

Outcomes served: product accepted, obligations satisfied.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Communication | shall establish and maintain communication with the supplier - interfaces, schedule, agendas, messages, documents, meetings and joint reviews - as the agreement defines |
| Communication record | shall record the communication with the supplier, the agenda and the minutes of each meeting included |
| Technical progress | shall exchange information with the supplier regularly on the technical progress of the deliveries, the risks to their successful completion included |
| Performance | shall check the supplier's progress against the agreement regularly - technical characteristics, quality, cost, dates - so that the constraints on them are met |
| Deviations | shall name each deviation and risk and discuss it with the supplier |
| Duties | shall check that every party meets its duties under the agreement, the acquirer included |
| Reviews and audits | shall monitor the supplier's progress through the reviews and audits the agreement and the acquisition plan define |
| Joint reviews | shall hold the joint reviews with the supplier on the acquirer's side and record each |
| Audit | shall commission the audit of the supplier and receive its result; the quality assurance process conducts the audit |
| Verification and validation | should supplement the monitoring, where needed, with the verification and validation of the supplier's products, done by those processes |
| What the acquirer owes | shall name what the acquirer owes the supplier, and each open item with whom it is owed to and by when |
| Data | shall provide the data the supplier needs in time and within the limits the agreement sets: data beyond them reaches a party with no obligation on its use |
| Problems | shall resolve problems in time |

## Payment and closure

Outcomes served: obligations satisfied.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Payment | shall make the payment or the other agreed action as the agreement defines |
| Closure | shall close the agreement once the payment or the other agreed action is done, the deliveries are accepted and no open item remains, each resolved as both parties agreed |
| Open items | shall name each item that keeps the agreement open |
| Notice of completion | should send the supplier a notice of completion, which is the record of closure |
| Portfolio | should pass the record of closure to the portfolio management process, which closes the project |
