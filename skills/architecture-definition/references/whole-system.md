# The architecture of a system defined whole

## Preparing for architecture definition

Outcomes served: concerns addressed.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Iteration | should iterate architecture definition together with business or mission analysis, stakeholder needs and requirements definition and requirements definition, to reach an agreed understanding of the problem and a satisfactory solution |
| Levels | may apply architecture definition at each level of the system hierarchy, with the detail the decisions of that level need |
| Drivers | shall review the pertinent information and identify the key drivers of the architecture |
| Their sources | should identify the drivers taking into account market studies, industry projects, competitors' plans and scientific results; the organization's strategy, policies and directives, regulatory and legal constraints and the stakeholder requirements; the mission or business concept, the operational concept of the system, its operational environment, the technology roadmap and the system requirements |

## Developing models and views of candidate architectures

Outcomes served: context defined.

The agent should build the context mainly from the results of business or mission analysis, at the
same time as stakeholder needs and requirements definition, taking as external entities the
existing and planned systems, products and services, enabling systems included, and defining the
interactions with them to the degree significant architecture decisions need, for design definition
to refine. The models and views of the candidates are developed as
`references/views-and-models.md` sets.

## Relating the architecture to design

Outcomes served: concerns addressed, elements defined, aligned with the requirements.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Top level | shall define the top level of the system architecture, identifying its items of hardware, software and manual operations, allocate all the system requirements among them, identify the configuration items from them, and record the architecture and the requirements allocated |
| Reference architecture | should, where the system elements stay abstract until their designs are done, express the architecture intent and check the feasibility of the design by a reference architecture made of system elements |
| Principles | shall define the principles of system design and evolution and record them |
| Human-centred design | shall define and perform human-centred design activities, incorporating human factors and ergonomic knowledge, methods and tools into the system design |
| Software items | should apply `references/software-item.md` to each software item the system architecture identifies |
