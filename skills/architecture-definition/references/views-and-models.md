# Viewpoints, models and views

A viewpoint is the conventions for the construction, interpretation and use of a view; an
architecture framework is the conventions, principles and practices for describing architectures in a given domain.

## Developing architecture viewpoints

Outcomes served: viewpoints developed, views developed.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Viewpoints | shall select, adapt or develop the viewpoints and model kinds on the basis of the stakeholder concerns, the user's viewpoint on the solution space included |
| Framework | shall establish or define the potential architecture framework or frameworks for the models and views |
| Its kind | should choose viewpoints that define the model kinds and how the models prepare the views, and a framework that names the stakeholders, their concerns and the viewpoints addressing them, or a general one |
| Justification | shall justify and record the selection of the framework, the viewpoints and the model kinds |
| Techniques and tools | shall select or develop the modelling techniques and tools |

## Developing models and views of candidate architectures

Outcomes served: views developed, concerns addressed.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Models | shall select, adapt or develop the models of the architecture candidates |
| Their kinds | should use the models that best capture the stakeholder concerns, logical and physical ones included |
| Views | shall compose from the models the views that show how the architecture addresses the stakeholder concerns and meets the system requirements, and record them |
| Consistency | shall make the models and views consistent with each other |
| Correspondence rules | should consider the correspondence rules of the architecture framework as one way of making the views consistent |
