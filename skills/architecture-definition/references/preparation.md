## Preparing for architecture definition

Outcomes served: candidates evaluated, enabling systems available.

The roadmap shows how the architecture evolves towards its intended end state, which can lie
beyond the lifetime of the current system; the approach is how the work is done - how the
stakeholders take part, how the results are examined, where the work is done; the strategy is the
systematic plan that carries out the approach along the roadmap.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Strategy | shall define the roadmap, the approach and the strategy of architecture definition |
| Its content | should include in the strategy the contractual capabilities of the stakeholders, the actions on the architecture views, the evaluation approach and criteria, and the measurement approach and methods, which the measurement process provides |
| Maintenance | shall maintain the recorded architecture definition and evaluation strategy, at each change of the architecture it governs |
| Its decision | should present a strategy it has defined with the output of the work for a decision |
| Enabling systems | shall identify and plan the enabling systems and services architecture definition needs: a modelling tool, a store of architecture information |
| Their requirements | should define the requirements for the enabling systems and the interactions with them, covering the tools for developing and evolving the architecture and the stores of information for its reuse: architecture patterns, artifacts, reference architectures |
| Access | shall obtain or acquire access to the enabling systems and services to be used |
| Validation | should have the validation process confirm objectively that an enabling system achieves its intended use |
