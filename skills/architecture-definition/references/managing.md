# Managing the architecture

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| formalizing the approach to managing the architecture, and the assignment of roles and authority in it | - | Approach |
| the formal agreement of the stakeholders to the architecture | - | Agreement, for the other stakeholders |

The agent shall take an explicit request as the formal agreement of a stakeholder only where the
user is that stakeholder.

## Managing the selected architecture

Outcomes served: concerns addressed, baseline achieved.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Approach | shall, once for the project and at each change of it, formalize the approach to managing the architecture and define the roles, responsibility, accountability and authority in it: for design, quality assurance, safety, security |
| Agreement | shall obtain the formal agreement of the stakeholders to the architecture from the stakeholders themselves |
| Confirmation | should have the verification and validation processes confirm that the models and views capture the stakeholder concerns and the system requirements |
| Certification | should, where the architecture is to be certified, have the certification confirm that the architecture intent is satisfied, the vision and the concepts are realized and the concerns are taken into account, and take its conclusions back into architecture definition |
