# Configuration evaluation

The status reports these rules check are those of `references/status-accounting.md`.

## Configuration evaluation

Outcomes served: audits performed.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Product configuration | shall verify the product configuration against the configuration requirements, the interface documents and the other requirements of the agreement |
| Verification results | should compare the requirements, the constraints and the variances with the results of formal verification, which the verification process produces |
| The process | shall evaluate the configuration management processes and maintain them so that the approved changes are carried out |
| Adequacy | should confirm by that evaluation that the plans and the processes are adequate and maintainable and that the approved changes are taken into account |
| Functional configuration audit | shall confirm that the system satisfies the functional baseline and implements its capabilities, and that the software items are functionally complete against their specified requirements |
| Physical configuration audit | shall confirm that the system conforms to its operational and configuration information items, and that the structure and the code of the software items reflect the current technical description: the release is built from what the baseline records |
| Results | shall record the results of the audits and the supervision and the dispositions on the items in the configuration management record |
| Completeness and consistency | shall check that the information on the items and their structures in the status reports is complete, and that the items are consistent with each other |
