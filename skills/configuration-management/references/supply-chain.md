# Under an agreement and along the supply chain

The baselines these rules speak of are defined by the rules of `references/identification.md`.

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| establishing a baseline with the supplier under an agreement | the change is not approved by the authority the configuration management strategy names; its verification is not known to have passed; an audit has found a discrepancy; an identifier that marks a baseline takes the conditions of the baseline; a condition that can be checked before the act but is not confirmed counts as unmet; a condition that can be checked only at acceptance is named and left to the acceptance checks | Acquirer's agreement |

Where the agreement gives the approval of a baseline to the acquirer, the agent shall take an
explicit request as that approval only where the user is the acquirer.

## Planning configuration management

Outcomes served: requirements distributed.

The strategy should define how configuration management is coordinated across the organizations of
the acquirer, the suppliers and the supply chain, and cover the life of the system or the scope of
the contract. The agent shall pass the configuration management requirements along the supply chain
to the suppliers whose elements are part of the system.

## Configuration identification

Outcomes served: baselines established.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Acquirer's agreement | shall obtain the acquirer's agreement before a baseline is established with the supplier |
| Analyses | should reach that agreement through the analyses of the project assessment and control process |

## Configuration change management

Outcomes served: changes managed.

The agent shall record a proposal for a change of a system element or of information under the
agreement as a change request of `SKILL.md`.
