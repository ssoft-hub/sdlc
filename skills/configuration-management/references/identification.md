# Configuration identification

A baseline established with a supplier under an agreement also takes the rules of
`references/supply-chain.md`. A change to the set of items is an act of the table in
`references/planning.md`.

## Configuration identification

Outcomes served: items managed, baselines established.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Items | shall define as configuration items the system elements and the information items that need to be identified, stored, tested, reviewed, used, changed, delivered or maintained on their own, the infrastructure elements, the knowledge assets, and the documentation with its source data and its transformation tools included |
| Identifiers and audits | should give each configuration item a unique identifier and make it the subject of configuration analyses and audits; products, system elements, information items and baselines are usually under control |
| Structure | shall define the hierarchy and the structure of the system information |
| Hierarchy | should name in that structure the hierarchy of the products or the system elements and the decomposition of the system |
| Files and directories | may create a structure of files, directories and hierarchies for access and storage |
| Identification scheme | shall establish the identifiers of the system, of its elements and of the information items, and a scheme for identifying the software items, and control their versions in the project |
| Conventions | should, where acceptable, distinguish items by unique long-term identifiers or by marking after the standards and conventions of the industry, such as the versioning scheme of the product's ecosystem, so that each item traces unambiguously to its specification or description |
| Baselines | shall define the baselines throughout the life cycle: the internal and the delivered baselines established by gathering every item they need, and, for each software item and its versions, the documentation that establishes the baseline, the references to the versions and the other identification data |
| Content of a baseline | should make a baseline combine the evolving states of the items at a defined time or circumstance as the basis of the next change, cover the related products - requirements, design and user documentation, test specifications -, and baseline the requirements, designs, plans and products; the functional, the allocated and the product baseline differ in scope or strategy |
