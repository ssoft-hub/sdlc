# The operation process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | the system is used to deliver its services: the software product is used in its intended environment, its correct and efficient operation ensured, and its customers supported at an acceptable level of service; the process establishes the requirements for operation and assigns it to personnel, monitors the services and the work of the operators, and identifies and analyses the operational anomalies against the agreements, the stakeholder requirements and the organizational constraints | - |
| constraints identified | the operation constraints that influence the system requirements, the architecture or the design are identified | the constraints recorded and passed to the system/software requirements definition, architecture definition and design definition processes |
| enabling systems available | the enabling systems, services and materials operation needs are available | access obtained to each enabling system planned, the resources applied as operation requires |
| operators available | trained, qualified operators are available | the operator functions assigned to personnel meeting the qualification and training requirements |
| services delivered | system services that meet the stakeholder requirements are delivered; the operation strategy is defined, the conditions of correct operation of the software defined and evaluated, the software tested and configured in its environment and operated in it, and the product put into operation as its requirements set | the strategy recorded, each release admitted by its operational testing, the services meeting their requirements |
| performance monitored | the system performance during operation is monitored; the risks of operation are identified and monitored and the criteria for operational use developed | the monitoring records, each case out of limits and each incident recorded, the risks recorded, the criteria recorded |
| customer supported | support to the customer is provided: assistance and consultation under the agreement, the customer's service needs identified and tracked, the satisfaction with the product and the service assessed on an ongoing basis, operation supported by handling the requests and resolving the problems, the support needs met by suitable services | the requests and the actions that followed recorded, the customer satisfaction data and report |
