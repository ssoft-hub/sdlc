# The software run as a service

The criteria for operational use stand in `references/release.md`.

## Preparing for operation

Outcomes served: performance monitored.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Risks of use | shall identify and record the risks of the product's use, monitor them continuously during its putting into service and its operation, and pass them to the risk management process in a risk analysis report and a risk category report |

## Performing operation

Outcomes served: services delivered, enabling systems available, performance monitored, customer
supported.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Use | shall use the system in its intended operational environment under the operation strategy and the user documentation, delivering services that meet the stakeholder requirements |
| Resources | shall apply the materials and the other resources - computing resources, energy, conditions for the operators - that operating the system and sustaining its services require |
| Monitoring | shall monitor the operation, comparing the operational characteristics and the services regularly with the defined criteria to learn of problems that may affect the service level, and record the results |
| Its content | should make the monitoring support the operation strategy, operate the system safely, with the occupational safety and environmental protection the law sets, analyse the strategy's measures to confirm that the performance and service parameters stay within acceptable limits, analyse the work within the established boundaries, the calibration of the instruments, the services provided and the response time, and use the operators' feedback and suggestions to improve operation |
| Cost | should monitor, at the period the strategy sets, the cost of operation against its goals and constraints and identify the potential for improvement |

## Managing operation results

Outcomes served: services delivered.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Traceability | shall maintain the traceability of the operation elements |
| Its extent | should keep it bidirectional over the life cycle, to the business or mission needs, the operational concept, the system's concept of operations and the stakeholder requirements |
