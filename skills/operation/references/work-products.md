# Work products of operation

The outcomes are those `references/process.md` names. "This process" as a source or a receiver is
operation at another of its steps. The parties are not processes: the acquirer, the other party to
the agreement; the customers `references/support.md` names; the operators and the users; the
supplier organization, which supplied the product.
