# Supporting the customers

The customers are the acquirer and the users of the services of the system.

## Managing operation results

Outcomes served: customer supported.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Temporary solution | shall make a temporary solution of a reported problem available to whoever reported it, with the conditions of its use, until the permanent correction, which goes through the maintenance process, is released |

## Supporting the customer

Outcomes served: customer supported.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Assistance | shall provide assistance and consultation to the customers and users at their request - training, documentation and the other support services the effective use of the product needs -, under the terms of the agreement |
| Its content | should include in the assistance recommended sources of training, documentation, solutions for vulnerabilities and counterfeits, and other support services |
| Service | shall establish, where none is established, a service through which the customer reports problems, raises questions and receives assistance, record and monitor the requests and the actions that follow, and identify and track the customer's service needs |
| Requests | shall forward user requests, where needed, to the maintenance process for resolution, inform the originators of the actions planned and taken, and monitor each resolution for its effectiveness |
| Satisfaction | shall determine, at the period the strategy or the agreement sets, to what degree the system's services satisfy the customers' needs, assessing on an ongoing basis their satisfaction with the product quality and the service provided |
| Its sources | may base the assessment on such data as operational data, surveys, interviews and studies |
| Service level | shall report to the acquirer, as often as the agreement requires, the service level performance it requires |
| End users | should assess the satisfaction of both the customer and the end user where they differ |
| Its use | should analyse the results, identify the actions that restore and improve operation for long-term customer satisfaction, agree them with the stakeholders or their representatives where that is beneficial, and pass the satisfaction data to the quality management process |
| Retirement request | should request the system's retirement from the disposal process where that analysis finds its services can no longer satisfy the customers |
| Communication | shall communicate, at the period the strategy or the agreement sets, the customer satisfaction data to the supplier organization and, taking into account the personnel involved and the nature of the results, to the customer |
