# The operators

## Preparing for operation

Outcomes served: operators available.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Requirements | shall define the qualification and training requirements of the personnel operation needs |
| Assignment | shall have the operator functions assigned to trained, qualified personnel |
| Qualification | should include in the training and qualification the understanding of the system in its environment and a programme of learning fault detection and isolation, confirm the admission to work by the selection criteria - knowledge, skills, experience -, fit the scope of qualification to the system and its environment, obtain the operators' certification where regulations require it, and consider how the training methods affect the readiness of the services |
