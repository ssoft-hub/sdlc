# Preparing operation

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| setting or changing the operation strategy | - | Strategy, the plan recorded |
| acquiring access to an enabling system that costs money or binds the project by conditions | - | Access |

## Preparing for operation

Outcomes served: constraints identified, enabling systems available, services delivered, performance
monitored.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Strategy | shall define the operation strategy - the duties, the environments and the constraints of operation and support - and the operational standards, record the plan and carry it out |
| What is missing | should propose what a recorded strategy lacks, and present it with the output of the work for a decision |
| Service requirements | shall record the service requirements the strategy serves, as the acquirer sets them, and give the acquirer the requirements the strategy adds |
| Its content | should define in the strategy the approaches, the work schedules, the resources and what operation specifically requires |
| Its scope | should consider for the strategy the suitability of the services as they are delivered and withdrawn, with their coordination with the earlier, parallel or continuing services of other systems; the staffing and the schedules of the operators; the release and re-acceptance criteria and schedules of the modifications that sustain or enhance the services; the modes of operation, normal and for contingencies; the performance measures; the safety of the operators and of other persons; the protection of the environment and the sustainability of operation; the monitoring of changes in threats and its results |
| Constraints | shall identify and record the constraints operation puts on the system that are to enter the system requirements, the architecture or the design, and pass them to the system/software requirements definition, architecture definition and design definition processes |
| Enabling systems | shall identify and plan the enabling systems and services of operation, the requirements for them and the interfaces with them included |
| Access | shall obtain or acquire access to the enabling systems and services to be used |
| Their fitness | should have the validation process confirm objectively that an enabling system achieves its intended use |
| Working environment | shall define, evaluate and establish the conditions and the working environment for the correct operation of the software |
| Problem procedures | shall define the procedures for receiving, recording, resolving and tracking problems and for providing feedback |
| Testing procedures | shall establish the procedures for testing the software product in the operational environment, for entering problem reports and modification requests into the maintenance process, and for releasing the product into use |

Outcomes served: services delivered.

| Subject | What the agent does |
