# Eliciting needs and expressing requirements

## Defining stakeholder needs

Outcomes served: needs defined, requirements defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Sources | should elicit the needs directly from the stakeholders, from implicit needs on knowledge of the domain and the context, and from the records of earlier work, with functional analysis as an aid |
| Quality needs | should elicit the implicit non-functional quality requirements through quality characteristics and models |
| What it weighs | should take into account the needs of society, the constraints from the acquirer, and the capabilities and abilities of users and personnel |

## Transforming needs into stakeholder requirements

Outcomes served: requirements defined.

The agent should express the requirements in a model, textual or formal, oriented to the goals and
behaviour of the system in the context of its environment and operating conditions, with a model of
product quality and of quality requirements as an aid.
