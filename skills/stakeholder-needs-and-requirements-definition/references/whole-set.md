# Requirements defined whole

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| rejecting a need in down-selection | the requirement contradicts an accepted requirement, a constraint or a need of another stakeholder; is unrealizable or unverifiable | Down-selection; Agreement of `SKILL.md` |

## Preparing for needs and requirements definition

Outcomes served: stakeholders identified.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Roles | should check the list of stakeholders against the roles: users, operators, supporters and maintainers, developers, producers, trainers and disposers, the acquirer and the supplier, those responsible for external interacting entities, regulatory bodies, representatives of the public |
| Proxies | should select representatives or proxies where direct contact is unrealistic, such as for a consumer product or a product published for everyone: whoever speaks for the product's users |

## Defining stakeholder needs

Outcomes served: requirements defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Down-selection | shall prioritize the selected needs and down-select them |
| Support | should set the priorities with the help of the decision management process, and have the system analysis process evaluate the feasibility of the needs and the impact of other factors |

## Analysing needs and requirements

Outcomes served: requirements defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Question | should state to the system analysis process the analysis question: the problem, the goals, the fidelity required |
| Feasibility | should have the system analysis process evaluate the feasibility and the affordability of the requirements |
| Verification and validation | should apply verification and validation in the analysis |

## Managing needs and requirements definition

Outcomes served: requirements defined, agreement achieved, traceability established.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Approvals | shall obtain the agreement, with the necessary approvals, of all who work to the requirements under a contract |
| Supply | shall take the agreed set, where the project supplies under an agreement, as the basis for negotiating and agreeing supply |
| Review | shall review the requirements at the points of key decisions of the life cycle to take account of changed needs |
| Candidates | should propose the needs, the stakeholder requirements and the operational concepts as the candidates for the baseline, which the configuration management process establishes and maintains |
| What the records keep | should keep in the records of the requirements the baseline and the changes of the needs with their origin, so that the records ground the traceability to the decisions of business or mission analysis, to the system requirements and to the requirements for elements, serve as a source of knowledge for later systems, and answer stakeholders on the status of their requirements |
| Traceability over the life cycle | should keep the traceability in both directions throughout the life cycle - needs and requirements, stakeholders and sources, the organization's strategy, the business or mission problems and opportunities - and to the system solutions, in the place the project keeps the requirements |
