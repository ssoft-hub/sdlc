# Work products of stakeholder needs and requirements definition

The outcomes are those `references/process.md` names. Where this process is both the source and the receiver,
the work product is the version in force that the process updates. The acquirer is the other party
to the agreement, from which the customer's requests and requirements come; the stakeholders are the
individuals and organizations with an interest in the system, from whom needs are elicited and with
whom requirements are agreed; neither is a process.

## Inputs

| Work product | Outcomes it serves | Source |
|---|---|---|
| the preliminary life cycle concepts, the problem and opportunity space and the selected solution class | context defined | the business or mission analysis process |
| the commitment / agreement | context defined, constraints identified | the acquisition process; the supply process |
| the customer request | context defined, constraints identified, agreement achieved | the acquirer; the stakeholders |
| the validation constraints | constraints identified | the validation process |
| the stakeholder requirements | context defined, constraints identified, requirements defined, agreement achieved, traceability established | the supply process, for the acquirer's requirements; this process, for those in force |
| the analysis report: the feasibility and the impact of the needs, the feasibility and the affordability of the requirements | requirements defined | the system analysis process |
| the change request | agreement achieved | the stakeholders; the acquirer; the validation process |
| the traceability record | traceability established | the business or mission analysis process |
