# Conflicting and problematic requirements

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| resolving a conflict between the requirements of different stakeholders in favour of one of them | the requirement contradicts an accepted requirement, a constraint or a need of another stakeholder; is unrealizable or unverifiable | Conflicting classes, Problems; Agreement of `SKILL.md` |
| rejecting a need as unrealizable or contrary to a constraint | the requirement contradicts an accepted requirement, a constraint or a need of another stakeholder; is unrealizable or unverifiable | Problems; Agreement of `SKILL.md` |

## Preparing for needs and requirements definition

Outcomes served: requirements defined, agreement achieved.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Conflicting classes | should seek, between classes of stakeholders whose interests conflict with each other but not with the system, agreement on a common set of acceptable requirements |
| Parties against the system | should leave unsatisfied the intentions of a stakeholder acting against the system, such as an adversary, and pass them to risk management, threat analysis, the system analysis process or the requirements for security, adaptability and resilience |

## Analysing needs and requirements

Outcomes served: requirements defined, agreement achieved.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Problems | shall resolve the problems of the requirements |
| Their kind | should treat as such problems the requirements that break a characteristic of a requirement or of the set, cannot be implemented or are impractical |
| Agreed resolution | should agree with a stakeholder a resolution that changes or rejects its requirement |
| Explanations | should explain conflicting, impractical and unrealizable requirements and reach agreement on the proposals for them |

## Managing needs and requirements definition

Outcomes served: agreement achieved.

The agent should confirm in the agreement that resolving conflicts has not distorted the
stakeholders' intentions.
