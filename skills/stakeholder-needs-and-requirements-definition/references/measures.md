# Measures of effectiveness and critical performance measures

## Defining stakeholder needs

Outcomes served: needs defined.

The agent should establish for the needs of the key stakeholders the measures of effectiveness by
which the characteristics of the system can be measured and evaluated.

## Analysing needs and requirements

Outcomes served: measures defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Critical performance measures | shall define and record the critical performance measures by which technical achievement is assessed |
| Their content | should define for each measure of effectiveness in the requirements the technical measures, the quality measures and the critical performance parameters, and define, analyse and examine the key measures to show conformance to the requirements and to identify the risks to cost, dates and operation from nonconformance |
