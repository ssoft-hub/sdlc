# Context of use, concepts and scenarios

## Defining stakeholder needs

Outcomes served: context defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Context of use | shall define and record the context of use within the concept of operations and the preliminary life cycle concepts: who uses the system, for what, in what environment and conditions |
| Its sources | should take the preliminary life cycle concepts from the business or mission analysis process rather than define them anew, and describe the context of use under the standards for describing it |

## Developing the operational concept and other life cycle concepts

Outcomes served: context defined, needs defined, requirements defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Concepts | shall develop the operational concept and the other life cycle concepts, and record them |
| Scenarios | shall define and record a representative set of operation and support scenarios that identifies all the required capabilities, the services |
| Other concepts | should develop the concepts of acquisition, installation, support, safety and disposal from the preliminary concepts, in the context of the needs, the scenarios and the interactions |
| What scenarios reveal | should use the scenarios to identify the needs no stakeholder has named - legal, regulatory and social obligations -, analyse the context of use - the actions of users, the characteristics of end users such as training and fatigue, the physical environment, the equipment, the social and organizational influences -, focus the scenarios on threats, environment, tools, methods and capabilities, and prioritize them by the weight of the operational needs |
| Updates | should update the concepts from the scenarios, and derive from misuse and failure scenarios the additional functional or derived requirements that reduce the risks those scenarios identify |
| User interaction | shall define the interaction of users with the system, taking into account human capabilities and the limits of human skills |
| Usability | should define in the usability requirements, as a minimum, the most effective, efficient and reliable characteristics of human work and of human interaction with the system, and, under ergonomics standards and accepted practice, the physical and mental abilities and the abilities to learn, the workplace, the environment and the means with other equipment, the normal, unusual and emergency conditions, and the recruitment, training and culture of operators and users |
| Usability through the life cycle | should plan, define and implement the usability requirements through the life cycle processes where usability matters |
| Standards | may apply standards of ergonomics and of the human-centred process to usability |
