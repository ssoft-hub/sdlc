# The stakeholder needs and requirements definition process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | the requirements for a system are defined whose fulfilment provides the capabilities users and other stakeholders need in a defined environment: the stakeholders and their needs are identified, and the needs are analysed and transformed into a common set of stakeholder requirements that expresses the intended interaction of the system with its environment and is the reference against which each resulting capability is validated | - |
| stakeholders identified | the stakeholders of the system are identified | the stakeholders recorded, each with its interest |
| context defined | the required characteristics and context of use of the capabilities and the life cycle concepts, operational concepts included, are defined | the context of use, the scenarios and the life cycle concepts recorded |
| constraints identified | the constraints on the system and its solutions are identified | the constraints recorded, each with its source |
| needs defined | the stakeholder needs are defined | the needs recorded, each with its source, rationale and importance |
| requirements defined | the stakeholder needs are prioritized and transformed into clearly defined stakeholder requirements, which are the basis for defining the system requirements and for validating the services | the requirements recorded, each leading to a need, with the results of their analysis, and the requirements basis in a baseline |
| measures defined | critical performance measures are defined | the measures recorded with the measures of effectiveness they derive from |
| agreement achieved | the stakeholders agree that their needs and expectations are adequately reflected in the requirements, and the agreed set is the basis for negotiating and agreeing supply | the confirmation of each stakeholder and the approvals of all who work to the requirements recorded |
| enabling inputs defined | the inputs for the requirements for any enabling systems or system elements the definition needs are defined | the inputs for the requirements for the enabling systems defined |
| enabling systems available | the enabling systems and services the definition needs are available | access obtained to each enabling system and service the definition uses |
| traceability established | traceability of the stakeholder requirements to the stakeholders and their needs is established | each requirement traced to its stakeholder, its need and the source of the need |
