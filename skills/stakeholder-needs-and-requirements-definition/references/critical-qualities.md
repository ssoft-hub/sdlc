# Safety, security and other critical qualities

## Defining stakeholder needs

Outcomes served: needs defined.

The agent should understand the needs for the minimum security and privacy protection the
operational environment requires.

## Transforming needs into stakeholder requirements

Outcomes served: requirements defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Critical qualities | shall define the requirements and functions for critical quality characteristics - safety, security, environment, health - and take into account the possible harmful effects of the use of the system on human health and safety |
| Safety | should identify the safety risks - of the methods of operation and support, to health, to property, to the environment - and establish the safety requirements and functions on their basis, under the applicable standards and practice |
| Security | should identify the security risks, consider the domains of security - physical, procedural, communications, computer, software, data, emanations -, identify the functions affecting security - access authorization, harm to personnel, property and information, compromise of sensitive information, denial of authorized access - and define the protection functions needed, reduction and containment of threats included |
