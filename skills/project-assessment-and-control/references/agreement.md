# Work under an agreement

## Assessing the project

Outcomes served: assessment results available, progress analysed, deviations analysed.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Performance | shall assess the project's performance under the agreement as well: cost, dates and work |
| Baseline | shall obtain through an analysis the acquirer's consent to a baseline that the configuration management process asks for |

## Controlling the project

Outcomes served: actions defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Change of the agreement | shall define the action to change the agreement when changes in cost, time or quality affect it |
| Its content | should consider in that action changed delivery dates and terms, or the selection of a new supplier |
| Its passing | shall pass a change request to the acquisition or the supply process, which decides it, for a change that requires a correction of the agreement |
