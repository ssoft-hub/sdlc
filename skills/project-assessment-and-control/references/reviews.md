# Planning assessment and holding reviews

## Acts of this situation

A change of direction, of a plan or of resources that a review brings: the acts table of
`SKILL.md`.

## Planning assessment and control

Outcomes served: assessment results available, progress analysed.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Strategy | shall define a strategy for the assessment and control of the project |
| Its content | should name in it the assessment and control activities expected: the methods and schedule of the assessments, and the management and technical analyses needed |

## Assessing the project

Outcomes served: resources assessed, progress analysed, deviations analysed, status communicated,
actions defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Analyses required | shall have the management and technical analyses, audits and inspections the project requires conducted under the direction of this process, each audit with its audit report |
| Who holds them | shall pass the technical reviews and inspections of the project's own products to verification, the audits to quality assurance, and the joint reviews with the other party to an agreement to acquisition or supply; this process holds the management reviews |
| Goal not achieved | shall define the corrective and preventive actions a review needs when it finds a goal of the project not achieved |
| Use of an analysis | should use an analysis, formal or informal, to determine readiness for the next stage or milestone, to help meet the project and technical goals, and to obtain feedback from the stakeholders |

The agent shall apply the rows below to a management review, not to an informal analysis.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Dates | shall hold the project management reviews at the dates the project plans define in advance |
| Their setting | shall set the dates, scope and participants of the reviews by the project's needs, and record them in the review plan |
| Ad hoc reviews | should hold the ad hoc reviews the stakeholders find needed beyond the planned ones |
| Other parties | may let other parties take part in an ad hoc review by agreement |
| Resources | shall provide all a review needs: personnel, location, service facilities, hardware and software, tools |
| Agenda | shall have the participants of each review agree on the agenda, the products and problems considered, the scope and procedures, and the entry and exit criteria |
| Material | shall collect, prepare and distribute the review material |
| Holding | shall evaluate at a management review the state of the project against the plans, schedules, standards and guidelines, and record the results as planned |
| Outcome to management | shall present the outcome to management for its decisions: activating the work under the plan, keeping the project under management by allocating resources, changing its direction or planning further, and evaluating and guiding the resolution of the risks that threaten its success |
| Review report | shall analyse the review report: identify and record the risks and problems, propose solutions on the results and set the priority of the actions |
| Problem resolution | shall pass the problems, where required, to problem resolution, which quality assurance carries |
| Results | shall record the results in the review record, with the evaluation of the review's adequacy - acceptance, non-acceptance or conditional acceptance - in the acceptance record, and distribute them |
| Access to results | shall keep the results available to all participating and interested parties |
| Action items | shall have the participants agree on the outcome of the review, the responsibility for the action items and the criteria for closing them |
| Closure | shall track the actions on the problems found to closure in the corrective action register, and report and record the changes of work products and processes |
