# Problems passed by other processes

## Controlling the project

Outcomes served: assessment results available, progress analysed, deviations analysed, status
communicated, actions defined.

| Source process | What it passes | What the agent does with it |
|---|---|---|
| quality management | a corrective action within the project that it assigns | shall handle it as a problem of the assessment |
| risk management | a selected risk response passed on as a problem | shall handle it as a problem of the assessment |
| quality assurance | a response to a problem that changes the plan, the resources or the schedule | shall handle it as a problem of the assessment |
| implementation, integration, verification, transition, validation, operation, maintenance | their problems and deviations | shall analyse the root cause of each, take the actions for correction or improvement, and resolve the problem together with the quality assurance process |
| the same processes | the lessons learned from those problems | shall record them by the row Lessons of `references/completion.md` |
