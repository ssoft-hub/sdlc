# The project assessment and control process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | the plans are balanced and feasible, the status of the project, of its technical performance and of its processes is determined, and the execution is directed according to the plans and schedules, within the budget and the technical parameters; the progress and achievements of the project are assessed periodically and at major events against the requirements, plans and business goals, and, on significant variances, information is prepared for management and the activities and tasks of the project are redirected to correct the deviations; the reviews maintain with the stakeholders a common understanding of the progress against the objectives of the agreement and of what is to be done to develop a product that satisfies them, at the project management level and at the technical level throughout the life of the project | - |
| assessment results available | performance measures or assessment results are available | the status of the project and the results of its assessments recorded where the project names |
| roles assessed | the adequacy of roles, responsibility, accountability and authority is assessed | the assessment of roles and authority recorded with its findings |
| resources assessed | the adequacy of resources is assessed | the assessment of the adequacy and suitability of resources recorded with its findings |
| progress analysed | technical analyses of the project's progress are performed | the record of each management review and of each analysis the project requires |
| deviations analysed | deviations of the work from the plans are investigated and analysed | each significant deviation recorded with its investigation and analysis |
| status communicated | the project's status is communicated to the stakeholders | each status report and progress report communicated to the stakeholders the agreement and the procedures name |
| actions defined | corrective actions are defined and directed, or replanning is performed | each problem recorded with its solution in the problem record and the corrective action register, and each action on a problem a review found tracked to closure |
| progress agreed | the actions to progress, or not to progress, to the next milestone or event are agreed | progress to the next milestone authorized or withheld by a decision that names the achievement criteria it rests on |
| goals achieved | the project's goals are achieved | the achievement of the project's goals recorded, and the decision declaring the project complete |
