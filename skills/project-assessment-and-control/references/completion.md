## Controlling the project

Outcomes served: goals achieved.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Goals | shall record the achievement of the project's goals |
| Completeness | shall determine, when all the software products, activities and tasks are completed, whether the project is complete by the criteria of the contract or of the organization's procedure |
| Archive | shall have the outputs and reports of the project archived in the environment the contract defines, by the information management process |
| Lessons | shall record the lessons learned and the data of the project, so that they are available to future projects and to process improvement, for the knowledge management and life cycle model management processes to distribute them |
