# Work products of project assessment and control

The outcomes are those `references/process.md` names. Where this process is both the source and the
receiver, the work product is the version in force that the process updates, or the previous one.
The acquirer is the external party to an agreement; organization management is a party within the
organization; the project's stakeholders are parties outside the project or within the organization;
the participating parties of a review are those who agreed on it. None of them is a process.
