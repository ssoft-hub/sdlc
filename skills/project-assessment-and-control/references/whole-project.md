# The project assessed whole

## Assessing the project

Outcomes served: assessment results available, roles assessed, resources assessed, deviations
analysed.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Plans | shall assess the management and technical plans for adequacy, balance and feasibility against the goals |
| Requirements | shall evaluate the plans and the software products for meeting the established requirements |
| Interfaces | shall track the status and changes of the interfaces between the project's elements and with other projects and organizational units |
| Monitoring | shall monitor the project's performance as a whole |
| Reports | shall present at the agreed dates, in the project status report, progress reports against the plan - internal, and those the contract requires for the acquirer -, showing adherence to the plans and the solutions where work stopped |
| Roles | shall assess the adequacy of roles, responsibility, accountability and authority, and record the assessment with its findings |
| Competence | should cover in that assessment the adequacy of the personnel's competence for the project's roles and tasks, and base it, where possible, on objective measures: of resource use, of project achievements |
| Resources | shall assess the adequacy and suitability of resources, and record the assessment with its findings |
| Their scope | should cover in that assessment the infrastructure, personnel, funding, time and whatever else is needed, and confirm that the commitments within the organization are met |
| Technologies | shall monitor the critical processes and new technologies |
| Maturity | should determine and assess the maturity of a technology and include it in the plans |
| Measurements | shall analyse the results of measurements and give recommendations on them; the measurement process collects, stores and analyses the data |
| Their analysis | should identify in the analysis the deviations, changes and undesirable trends from the planned values, potential problems included, and direct the recommendations to correction and prevention |
| Statistics | should apply statistical analysis to the measures where acceptable: trends, error rate, distribution of parameters, repeatability of the process |
| Processes | shall monitor the performance of the processes in the project |
| Process measures | should analyse in that monitoring the process measures and their trends against the project's goals |
| Who monitors | may leave that monitoring to the quality assurance process or the life cycle model management process |
