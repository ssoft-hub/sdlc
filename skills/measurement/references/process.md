# The measurement process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | objective data and information on the products, services and processes are collected, analysed and reported, to support effective management and to demonstrate their quality | - |
| needs defined | the information needs of the technical and management processes are defined | the information need named for each measurement; for the project, the needs defined with their priority |
| measures defined | a set of measures based on the information needs is defined or developed | each measure recorded with the information need it serves |
| data collected | the measurement activities are defined and planned, and the needed data are collected, checked and stored | the data stored with their context: when, where from and how each value was obtained; the strategy and the procedures defined, or the project's rules serving as them |
| data analysed | the data are analysed and the results interpreted | the information product with its interpretation |
| information provided | information products give objective information to support decisions, and the measures and the measurement process are evaluated and their improvements communicated | the results recorded and communicated to the measurement users, every value not measured named as an estimate; the feedback gathered; the evaluation, its problems recorded and passed to quality assurance, and the improvements passed to the process owners |
