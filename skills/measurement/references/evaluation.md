# Measurement evaluated and improved

## Evaluating measurement

Outcomes served: information provided.

The criteria of the evaluation are those of `references/planning.md`.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Evaluation | shall evaluate the information products and the measurement activities against the evaluation criteria, with respect to the information needs and the measurement strategy |
| Improvements | shall identify the possible improvements to measurement and communicate them to the owners of the processes concerned, the owner of the measurement process included |
| Deficiencies | shall record each deficiency the evaluation finds as a problem and pass it to quality assurance |
