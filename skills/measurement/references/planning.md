# Planning measurement

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| adopting or changing the measurement strategy | - | Strategy |
| approving the resources of measurement | - | Resources |
| acquiring and deploying supporting tools and services | - | Supporting tools |

## Preparing for measurement

Outcomes served: needs defined, measures defined, data collected, data analysed, information
provided.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Strategy | shall define the measurement strategy - how the measurement activities and results are defined, performed and evaluated, on the needs of the organization and of the project -, taking it from the project's rules, such as the measures its enabling systems collect, and naming what is missing, and plan the measurement activities |
| Organization | shall describe, with the strategy, the characteristics of the organization that bear on measurement: its processes, tools, data sources and constraints |
| Information needs | shall define, with the strategy, the information needs of the technical and management processes and set their priority |
| Grounds of the needs | should base the information needs on the business goals of the organization, the goals of the project, the risks identified and the other factors of the project's decisions |
| Set of measures | shall select or develop the measures that satisfy those needs and record them, the measures for evaluating the architecture included |
| Procedures | shall define the procedures for data collection, analysis, access and reporting |
| Evaluation criteria | shall define, with the strategy, the criteria for evaluating the information products and the measurement process, which `references/evaluation.md` applies |
| Resources | shall define and consider the resources for the measurement tasks, and provide them once approved |
| Supporting tools | shall define and plan the enabling systems or services the measurement needs - tools for collecting, storing and analysing data -, and provide for their acquisition and putting into service, carried out by the decision on them |

## Performing measurement

Outcomes served: data collected.

The agent shall integrate the procedures for generating, collecting and analysing the data and for
reporting into the relevant processes, such as collection during build and testing or in the
place the project keeps its problem reports. The agent shall collect with them the data of process and project measures that life cycle
model management and project assessment and control analyse. The agent should have the life cycle
processes those procedures are integrated into perform part of the measurement activities
themselves.
