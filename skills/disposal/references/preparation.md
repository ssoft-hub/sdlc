## Preparing for disposal

Outcomes served: constraints provided, enabling systems available.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Constraints | shall identify and record the constraints disposal puts on the system requirements, the architecture characteristics, the design properties and the implementation methods, and pass them as inputs to the system/software requirements definition, architecture definition, design definition and implementation processes |
| Their content | should cover in the constraints the problems of disassembly, the enabling systems concerned included, the access to the storage locations and their suitability, and the levels of proficiency required |
| Enabling systems | shall identify and plan the enabling systems and services disposal needs, their requirements and interfaces included, and pass those requirements to the infrastructure management process |
| Access | shall obtain or acquire access to the enabling systems and services to be used |
| Suitability | should have the validation process confirm objectively that an enabling system achieves its intended use |
