# End of support

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| the plan for ending the operation and the active support | - | Plan to Users |
| the date the support ends, and the notification of the users | the users are not notified | Notification |
| ending the parallel operation of the old and the new | the release has not passed the acceptance test the agreement requires; the users are not notified; other systems still depend on the one being withdrawn | Parallel operation; Its support; Notification |

Ending the support of an old environment after a migration is an act of the maintenance process.

## Preparing for disposal

Outcomes served: elements disposed.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Plan | shall develop and record the plan for ending the operation and the active support of the software by those who operate and maintain it |
| Its items | shall have the plan carry each item of the table below |
| Users | shall include the participation of the users in the planned actions |

| The plan for ending the support carries |
|---|
| the end of full or partial support after a set period |
| the archiving of the software product and its documentation |
| the responsibility for the remaining support matters |
| the transition to a new software product, where one is needed |
| open access to copies of the archived data |

## Performing disposal

Outcomes served: constraints provided, elements disposed, environment restored, reports available.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Notification | shall notify the users of the plans and activities for retiring the software products and services - the replacements or upgrades with their dates, why the product is no longer supported, the other support options once support ends -, recording each notification |
| Parallel operation | shall run the old and the new product in parallel where a new product replaces the old, while the old is removed and the new comes into use, for a smooth transition, leaving the installation of the new one to the transition process |
| Its support | shall support the transition and train the users during the parallel operation as the contract defines |
| Retirement | shall notify all concerned when the scheduled retirement arrives, recording each notification |
