# Operators

## Performing disposal

Outcomes served: reports available.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Operators | shall withdraw the operators from the element |
| Their withdrawal | should withdraw them under the requirements of safety, security, privacy and the environment |
| Operating knowledge | shall record the knowledge of operating the element |
| Its preservation | should have the knowledge management process preserve and protect the knowledge and skills of the operators |
| Their future | should leave the redeployment, the re-employment or the dismissal of the operators to the human resource management process |
