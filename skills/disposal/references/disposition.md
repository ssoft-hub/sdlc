# Disposition beyond deletion

For software, the supply chain is the channels through which the software reaches its users - a
place of publication, a download page, a store of images; keeping an element from returning to it
is, for instance, withdrawing a release from the place it is published in.

## Preparing for disposal

Outcomes served: elements disposed.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Storage | shall define the facilities, the storage locations, the inspection criteria and the storage periods, where the element is to be stored |
| Supply chain | shall define preventive methods that keep elements and materials not to be offered again, reclaimed or used from returning to the supply chains |

## Performing disposal

Outcomes served: elements disposed.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Requirements | should perform the disposition under the requirements of safety, security, privacy and the environment |
| Remaining life | should transfer elements with remaining useful life to other systems or organizations, repairing them where acceptable |
| Unfit elements | should keep an element unfit for reuse from returning to the supply chain |
| Other waste | should remove as well the waste material of production and of the other stages |
| Parts not reused | shall disassemble the elements and parts not intended for reuse so that it is confirmed they do not return to the supply chains |
| Destruction services | should obtain destruction services where the destruction needs them |
