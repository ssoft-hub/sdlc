# The system analysis process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | a rigorous basis of data and information is provided for the technical understanding of the system, on which the decisions of its life cycle rest: the inputs of technical assessment that give confidence in the applicability and integrity of the requirements, the architecture and the design | - |
| studies identified | the system studies needed are identified | each question recorded with its stakeholders, scope, goals, required fidelity and the methods selected |
| assumptions agreed | the assumptions of the analysis and its results are agreed | the assumptions recorded with the agreement of the stakeholders of the analysis, and the results recorded with them |
| results provided | the analysis results are provided for decisions | the analysis report passed to whoever decides on it, with its conclusions and recommendations, examined for quality and validity and traceable to the items it supports |
| inputs identified | the inputs for the requirements of the enabling systems or system elements the analysis needs are identified | those inputs recorded, such as the infrastructure requirements or the requirements for a prototype |
| enabling systems available | the enabling systems or services the analysis needs are available | access obtained to each enabling system planned |
