# The decision management strategy

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| setting or changing the decision management strategy | - | Strategy, Strategy record |

## Preparing for decisions

Outcomes served: decisions identified.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Strategy | shall define the decision management strategy: the roles, the responsibility, the accountability and the authority for decisions, the decision makers, and the organizational mechanisms a decision needs |
| Its content | should name in it the categories of decisions, the scheme of their priorities, and the rigor and formality of the analysis that the guidelines of the organization or of the project set |
| Sources of decisions | should take into account in it where decisions arise from: assessments of effectiveness, technical trade-offs, problems to be solved, a risk exceeding its acceptable level, new opportunities, and the move of the project to the next life cycle stage |
| Gaps | should propose, with the output of the work, what the project's strategy lacks |
| Strategy record | shall record the strategy, once the decision on it is taken, where the project keeps its rules |
