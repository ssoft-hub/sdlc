# The decision management process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | a structured analytical framework objectively identifies, characterizes and evaluates the alternatives of a decision at any point of the life cycle and selects the most beneficial course of action; the decisions and their rationale are recorded to support future decisions | - |
| decisions identified | the decisions requiring analysis of alternatives are identified, and the decision management strategy is defined | the decision named with its circumstances and category before the work selects an alternative; the strategy recorded, or the rules of `SKILL.md` serving as it |
| alternatives evaluated | alternative courses of action are identified and evaluated | the criteria and the alternatives recorded, with the evaluation of each alternative against the criteria |
| course selected | the preferred course of action is selected | the selection by the decision maker, or by the agent within what `SKILL.md` permits it, of the alternative recommended or of another one |
| rationale recorded | the rationale of the decision and the assumptions are recorded, and the decision is communicated to the stakeholders | the decision record with the criteria, the alternatives considered, the rationale and the assumptions, and its communication to the stakeholders |
