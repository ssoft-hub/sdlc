# After the decision

## A choice met in the work

Outcomes served: decisions identified, alternatives evaluated, course selected, rationale recorded.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Plan change | shall pass a change request to the project planning process, which decides it, for a decision that changes the project plan |
| Passing | shall pass a decision on a supplier's proposal to supply and acquisition as the proposal review record, and the selection of a solution class to business or mission analysis |

## Managing the decision

Outcomes served: rationale recorded.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Tracking | shall track and evaluate a decision once it is implemented, and report whether the problem is resolved, the adverse trend corrected or the opportunity used |
| Result not achieved | shall name a result the work shows the decision has not achieved, together with that decision |
| A changed decision | should take a change of a decision as a new decision that refers to the one it changes, and keep the record of the earlier one |
