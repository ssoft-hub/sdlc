# A formal analysis of alternatives

## Preparing for decisions

Outcomes served: alternatives evaluated.

The agent should obtain from the stakeholders involved in the decision the expert evaluation that
the analysis and the decision require.

## Analysing the decision information

Outcomes served: alternatives evaluated.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Analysis needed | should define the system analysis and the data needed to evaluate the alternatives; the methods most often used are trade studies and engineering analysis |
| Criteria | should give each criterion a desired value, a threshold beyond which a value is unsatisfactory, and a weight |
| Scope of the criteria | should cover by the criteria, where they apply, the cost, the dates, the programmatic constraints, the regulations, the technical performance characteristics, the critical quality characteristics and the risks |
| Screening | should screen many alternatives qualitatively - by the impact of risks, the cost, the dates, the regulations - down to a number that can be analysed in detail |
| System analysis | should pass to the system analysis process, where needed, the quantification of the criteria, the detailed evaluation of a parameter against a criterion and the analysis of sensitivity over the range of the critical parameters, as an analysis question naming the problem, the goals and the fidelity required |
| Feasibility | should establish the feasibility of the alternatives from the results of that analysis |

## Making the decision

Outcomes served: course selected.

The agent should compare the alternatives against the selection criteria quantitatively, through
a selection model such as a weighted sum.
