## Acquiring and providing skills

Outcomes served: people provided, skills developed.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Team needs | shall define the needs of the projects and the organization for project teams, the structure of each team and its operating rules |
| Authority | shall give the teams the authority for their roles and record it |
| Team readiness | shall check that each team has an understanding of its role in the project, a shared vision of or interest in its success, the means of communication and interaction with other teams, and management support |
| Awareness | should see that each participant is aware of the significance of their work and its contribution to the project's quality goals |
| Interaction | shall obtain, record and maintain an agreement on managing the interactions between teams, keeping it as the communication record |
| Coordination skills | shall ensure that the workers have the skills of sharing information and coordinating their actions |
