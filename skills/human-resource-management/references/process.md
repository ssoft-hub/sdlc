# The human resource management process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | the organization is provided with the human resources it needs and their competence is maintained, consistent with its business needs: a supply of skilled and experienced personnel, qualified to perform the life cycle processes, to perform their roles effectively and to work together in teams, so that the goals of the organization, the project and the customer are achieved | - |
| skills identified | the skills required by the projects are identified | the skill needs of the current and expected projects recorded, and the skills of the personnel recorded |
| people provided | the necessary human resources are provided to the projects | each role or task a project needs filled by an assignment, with the team's structure, operating rules and authority recorded |
| skills developed | the skills of the personnel are developed, maintained or enhanced | the skills development strategy and the training plan recorded, the training given, and the records of skill development and of evaluations of work kept |
| conflicts resolved | conflicts in multi-project resource demands are resolved | each conflict over people between projects named with its options and settled by a decision |
