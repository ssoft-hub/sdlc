# Work products of human resource management

The outcomes are those `references/process.md` names. Where this process is both the source and the receiver,
the work product passes from one task of human resource management to a later one, or is the
version in force that the process updates. Organization management, the personnel and the
project teams with their management are parties within the organization, not processes. The
knowledge item, the asset use data and the knowledge management strategy belong to the knowledge
management process.

## Inputs

| Work product | Outcomes it serves | Source |
|---|---|---|
| the human resource policy | skills identified, people provided, skills developed | organization management |
| the human resource management plan in force | skills identified, people provided, skills developed | this process; the project's need for personnel comes from the resource requests the project planning process submits |
| the human resource needs analysis | skills identified, people provided, skills developed | this process, from the identification of skill needs |
| the knowledge repository | skills identified, people provided, skills developed | the knowledge management process |
| the training strategy in force | skills developed | this process |
| the training plan in force | people provided, skills developed | this process |
| the training material | skills developed | external training suppliers; the knowledge management process, for training materials that are knowledge assets |
| the training record in force | people provided, skills developed | this process |
| the training evaluation report | people provided, skills developed | this process, from the evaluation of the benefit of training |
| the personnel record in force | people provided, skills developed | this process |
