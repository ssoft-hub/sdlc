# Evaluating and motivating personnel

An evaluation uses the criteria of the step Criteria of `SKILL.md` and is kept by its steps Records
to Public artifacts.

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| evaluating a person's work | no objective criteria for the evaluation are known to be defined | Evaluation, Feedback |
| rewarding a person | - | Motivation, which the reward carries out |

## Acquiring and providing skills

Outcomes served: skills developed.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Evaluation | shall evaluate the work of personnel against the defined criteria, by its contribution to the goals of the project and the organization, and record the evaluation as the personnel performance evaluation, kept in the personnel evaluation record |
| Feedback | shall communicate the results of an evaluation to the person evaluated |
| Motivation | shall motivate the personnel, for example by career development opportunities and reward mechanisms |
