# Developing skills

## Identifying skills

Outcomes served: skills identified, skills developed.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Types and levels | shall identify the types and levels of training and knowledge that the projects and the organization need |
| Skills to develop | shall identify and evaluate the skills and competencies to be obtained or improved through development and training |

## Developing skills

Outcomes served: skills identified, people provided, skills developed.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Strategy | shall establish and record a strategy for developing skills and professional competence: how the competence needs are identified, how development and training are developed or acquired, and how they are delivered |
| Training plan | shall develop and record a training plan: the types and levels of training, the categories of personnel, the schedules, the resource requirements and the training needs |
| Resources | shall obtain or develop the resources for training, education or mentoring that meet the general skill development needs: training materials and guides of the organization or external parties, courses of external suppliers, electronic instruction |
| External training | shall pass the training to be obtained from an external supplier to the acquisition process as its acquisition plan: the training, the personnel, the dates. Entering into, changing or closing an agreement under which the project acquires is an act of the acquisition process. |
| Content | should state in the training it develops or obtains the goals, the curriculum, the materials and the other training resources it needs |
| Delivery | shall provide the planned skill development: train the personnel in the knowledge and skills their roles and the project need, the training that the knowledge management strategy plans for the contributors and users of knowledge assets included, and develop, maintain and enhance their skills |
| Records | shall maintain records of skill development: the training conducted and the actual skills |
| Benefit | shall determine, evaluate and record the benefit of each training, satisfaction with its results included, as the training evaluation report |
| Opportunities | shall identify and provide opportunities for developing the skills and competencies of the personnel |
