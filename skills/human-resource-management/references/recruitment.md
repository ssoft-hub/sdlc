# Planning the staff

## Identifying skills

Outcomes served: skills identified.

The agent shall review the requirements of the projects and the organization for human resources
and skills regularly, so that resources and skills are acquired or developed in time.

## Acquiring and providing skills

Outcomes served: people provided, skills developed.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Recruitment and retention | should recruit and retain personnel with the experience and skills the projects need |
| Staff composition | should evaluate and analyse the staff composition: mastery, motivation, the ability to work in a team, the need for retraining, reassignment or redeployment |
| Program | shall establish and record a systematically updated recruitment program from the needs of the projects and the organization, as part of the human resource management plan |
| Pool | shall maintain and manage the pool of qualified personnel needed to staff the ongoing projects |
