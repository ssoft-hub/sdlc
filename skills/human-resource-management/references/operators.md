# Operators

The training a release needs at its transition is left to the transition process.

## Acquiring and providing skills

Outcomes served: people provided.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Number | shall obtain the operators in the number that the operation process needs, by the step Obtaining of `SKILL.md` |
| Release | should redeploy to other projects, re-employ or dismiss the operators released when a system or an element of it is withdrawn from operation; each such assignment or dismissal is an act of the table of `SKILL.md` |

## Developing skills

Outcomes served: people provided, skills developed.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Training | shall train the operators by the qualification and training requirements that the operation process defines, by the step Delivery of `references/training.md` |
| Proficiency | should acquire, train and certify personnel as necessary to maintain the number and proficiency of operators that the maintenance process requires |
