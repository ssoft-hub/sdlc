## Preparing for business or mission analysis

Outcomes served: all seven (`references/process.md`).

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Strategy | shall define the business or mission analysis strategy |
| Its decision | should present a strategy it has defined with the output of the work for a decision |
| Its content | should name in the analysis strategy the approach to defining the problem space, to characterizing the solution space and to selecting the class |
| Enabling systems | shall identify and plan the enabling systems and services the analysis needs |
| Access | shall obtain or acquire access to the enabling systems and services |
| Their requirements | should define the requirements for the enabling systems and services and the interactions with them |
| Their fitness | should have the validation process confirm objectively that each enabling system achieves its intended use |
