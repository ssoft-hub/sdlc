# The business or mission analysis process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | the business or mission problems or opportunities are defined, the solution space is characterized, and the potential solutions that could resolve the problem or make it possible to take advantage of the opportunity are determined | - |
| problem space defined | the problem or opportunity space is defined | the problems and opportunities recorded with their context and key parameters, tied to no solution |
| solution space characterized | the solution space is characterized | the concepts and the alternative solution classes recorded that together cover the possible solutions |
| concepts defined | the preliminary operational concept and other life cycle concepts are defined | the operational concept recorded with its modes or states, scenarios and use cases, and the concepts of acquisition, operation, support and withdrawal from operation |
| classes analysed | alternative solution classes are identified and analysed | each class recorded with its evaluation against the criteria |
| class selected | the preferred solution class or classes are selected | the request naming the class, or the recorded decision selecting a class or rejecting all of them |
| enabling systems available | the enabling systems and services the analysis needs are available | access obtained to each enabling system and service the analysis uses |
| traceability established | traceability of the problems and opportunities to the preferred solution classes is established | each preferred class traced to the problem or the opportunity it resolves |
