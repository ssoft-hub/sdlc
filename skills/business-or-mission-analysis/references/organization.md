## Preparing for business or mission analysis

Outcomes served: problem space defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Scope | should cover in the analysis of the step Goals of `SKILL.md` the business or mission, the vision, the concept of operations and the other strategic goals of the organization, the deficiencies and bottlenecks in the existing capabilities of a system, product or service included |
| Strategy as context | should take the organization's strategy - its concept of operations, that is the intended ways of leadership in managing the organization, its strategic goals and plans, its new markets or mission elements, its known problems and opportunities - as the context of the analysis, and not define it |
| Assumptions | should name the organization's assumptions about the use of the system being developed and of the existing and future systems |
| The organization as the system | should treat the organization's strategy as part of the definition of the system where the system of interest is the organization itself |

## Defining the problem and opportunity space

Outcomes served: problem space defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Range of factors | should aim the analysis at the space and the direction of the solution, considering, beyond the trade-space factors of the product, changes in mission requirements, business opportunities and capabilities, and political, economic, social, technological, environmental and legal factors |
| Market report | should record an analysis of a market in the market analysis report |
| Methods | may carry out the analysis as an external or an internal analysis, or as an analysis of strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats |
| Portfolio | should pass the results of the analysis to the portfolio management process as part of its decisions |
| Capabilities | should, where the domain speaks of the capabilities of the organization, focus the analysis on the capabilities needed, determine the place in the trade space together with the portfolio management process, translate the problems and opportunities into target aspects of the system, and include the target capabilities in the problem or opportunity space |
