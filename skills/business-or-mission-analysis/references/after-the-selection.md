# After the selection

## Managing business or mission analysis

Outcomes served: problem space defined, solution space characterized, concepts defined, classes
analysed, class selected, traceability established.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Both directions | should maintain the traceability in both directions throughout the life cycle: between the problems and opportunities and the preferred classes, and with the organization's strategy, the stakeholder needs and requirements and the results of system analysis the decisions rest on |
| Configuration | should name the candidates for the baseline and provide the information items to the configuration management process, which establishes and maintains the configuration items and the baselines |
| Analysis repeated | should perform the analysis again for the problems and classes affected when the environment, the needs or other conditions the selected class rests on change |
