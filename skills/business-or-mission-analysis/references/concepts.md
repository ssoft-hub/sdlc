# The concepts in full

## Characterizing the solution space

Outcomes served: concepts defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Their content | should cover acquisition, operation, support and withdrawal from operation, and name in the operational concept the high-level modes or states, the operational scenarios and the possible use cases or the use within the proposed business strategy, so that the concepts serve the feasibility analysis and the evaluation of the alternatives |
| Stakeholder groups | should name the main stakeholder groups - customers, users, administrators, regulators - and pass them with the concepts to the stakeholder needs and requirements definition process, which identifies the groups in full and refines the concepts |
| Vulnerabilities | should understand, together with the product, the vulnerabilities of the operational environment related to known security threats, and investigate the vulnerabilities of the user's interaction with the system in the context of the critical mission |
