# The detailed design of a software item

## Design characteristics and design enablers of each element

Outcomes served: artifacts developed.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Database | shall develop and record a detailed design of the database, where the item has one |

## Managing the design

Outcomes served: characteristics defined, artifacts developed, traceability established.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Correctness and testability | shall analyse the detailed design and the test requirements for correctness and testability, so that the units can be built and tested |
| Evaluation | shall evaluate the detailed design and the test requirements for traceability to the requirements of the software item, external consistency with the architectural design, internal consistency between the components and the units, appropriateness of the design methods and standards, feasibility of testing, and feasibility of operation and maintenance |
| Results | shall record the results of the evaluation |
| Reviews | shall have the detailed design pass a technical review, which the verification process conducts, and a management review, which the project assessment and control process conducts |
