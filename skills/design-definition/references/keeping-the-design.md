# Keeping the design over the life cycle

## Managing the design

Outcomes served: artifacts developed, traceability established.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Extent of traceability | should keep the traceability bidirectional throughout the life cycle, between the design characteristics and the architectural entities, the interfaces, the analysis results, the verification methods and the requirements of the elements |
| Configuration | should have configuration management establish and maintain the baselines and the configuration items |
