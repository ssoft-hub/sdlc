## Preparing for design definition

Outcomes served: characteristics defined, enabling systems available.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Types of characteristics | shall define, for the system, the release or the kind of element prepared, the types of design characteristics needed |
| Per technology | should define the types for each technology: patterns, structures, size, volume, gauges, templates |
| Principles of evolution | shall define, for the system, the release or the kind of element prepared, the principles of design evolution |
| Their content | should provide in the principles for the periodic evaluation of the design characteristics as the system and its architecture evolve, and for the forecast of the obsolescence of components and technologies, of their replacement over the life cycle and of its consequences for the design |
| Strategy | shall develop the design definition strategy |
| Its decision | should present a strategy it has defined with the output of the work for a decision |
| Enabling systems | shall identify and plan the enabling systems and services design definition needs: collaboration and design tools, a store of design information |
| Their requirements | should define the requirements for the enabling systems and the interfaces with them, covering the tools for collaboration and design development and the stores of information for design reuse: design patterns, artifacts, standards |
| Access | shall obtain or acquire access to the enabling systems and services to be used |
| Validation | should have the validation process confirm objectively that an enabling system achieves its intended use |
