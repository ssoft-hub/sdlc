# Training and support of the people

The qualification and proficiency of the operators the project provides, beyond what a release
needs, are left to the human resource management process.

## Preparing for transition

Outcomes served: people trained.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Training defined | shall define and arrange the training of the operators, the users and the other parties the use and the support of the system need: whom, in what, by what means - an instruction, a manual, a session |

## Performing transition

Outcomes served: people trained, ready for operation.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Training | shall provide the training - initial and continuing, as the agreement defines - and support the acquirer in preparing for and starting the use of the software in its working environment |
| Training record | shall record the training given against the training defined |
| Support procedure | shall give the acquirer the customer support procedure: how the users and the operators get support, from whom and by what channel |
| Start of operation | should support the users and the operators while the system is put into service |
