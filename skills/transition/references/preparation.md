# Preparing transition

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| setting or changing the transition strategy and its dates | - | Strategy, recorded and agreed |
| acquiring access to an enabling system that costs money or binds the project by conditions | - | Access |

## Preparing for transition

Outcomes served: constraints identified, enabling systems available, installed and capable.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Strategy | shall define and record the transition strategy - the plan of installing the software product into its target environment, as the agreement defines -, agreed with the customer and the operating organization |
| What is missing | should propose what a recorded strategy lacks, and present it with the output of the work for a decision |
| Its content | should cover every activity from delivery to the site and installation through activation to putting into service under the agreements; the mechanisms preserving the system's integrity; the involvement of every stakeholder, the operator included; roles and responsibilities; facilities, shipping and receiving; plans for reverting in contingencies; training; the demonstration of the installation at acceptance; the operational readiness review; the start of operation; the criteria of a successful transition and its relation to other plans |
| Constraints | shall identify and record the constraints transition puts on the system that are to enter the system requirements, the architecture or the design, and pass them to the system/software requirements definition, architecture definition and design definition processes |
| Enabling systems | shall identify and plan the enabling systems and services of transition, the requirements for them and the interfaces with them included |
| Access | shall obtain or acquire access to the enabling systems and services to be used |
| Their fitness | should have the validation process confirm objectively that an enabling system achieves its intended use |
| Shipping and receiving | shall define and arrange the shipping and receiving of the system elements and the enabling systems: the delivery channel - a place of publication, a medium, a server - and who receives |

## Performing transition

Outcomes served: installed and capable.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Interim storage | should keep the product in interim storage before its delivery, where the delivery needs it |
| Staged delivery | should deliver in complete parts, where the delivery is staged |
