# A site prepared for the release

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| a change of the site that costs money or affects other parties' systems | the site is not prepared | Site changes; Installation criteria; Site |

Acquiring, putting into service, changing or withdrawing an infrastructure element is an act of the
infrastructure management process.

## Preparing for transition

Outcomes served: site prepared, installed and capable.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Site changes | shall identify and record the changes of the facilities or the site needed for the installation and for the use of the release |
| Installation criteria | shall develop from the installation requirements, and record, the criteria for the environment in which the software will be installed |
| Adaptation | shall specify the requirements for adapting the system to its environment: its configuration, its parameters, its connections with the other systems of the environment |

## Performing transition

Outcomes served: site prepared, installed and capable.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Site | shall prepare the site for operation under the installation requirements and the criteria for the environment |
| Its conduct | should prepare it in compliance with the health, safety and security requirements and the environmental instructions |
| Representative example | should demonstrate the installation on a representative example where the exact location or environment is not available |
