# Needs for maintenance

A modification request asks to change the software in use: corrective, removing a defect found;
adaptive, to a changed environment; perfective, improving it; preventive, removing the cause of a
failure before it occurs.

## Preparing for maintenance

Outcomes served: needs reported.

The agent should treat an expansion of capabilities, an upgrade or an enhancement of a legacy system
as a new project of development, with its own life cycle processes, rather than as a modification
in maintenance.

## Performing maintenance

Outcomes served: needs reported.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Incidents | shall analyse incidents and problems, those the operation process passes on included, to identify the future needs for corrective, adaptive, perfective and preventive actions |
| Adaptation | shall determine when adaptive or perfective maintenance is required |
| Its reach | should expect such maintenance to change the system requirements, the architecture or the design |
| New project | should leave the opening of a modification that needs a new project, with its work from the development stage, to the portfolio management process |
