# Logistics

For software, the logistics cover what its support needs beside the code: hosting and computing
resources, spare hardware, licences, support equipment, technical data - manuals, instructions,
lists -, people and their training, and the media the software and its data are kept and moved on;
the supply chains are the registries and vendors the replacements come from.

## Logistics support

Outcomes served: enabling systems available, elements available.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Acquisition logistics | shall perform acquisition logistics |
| In the agreement | should have the agreement account for acquisition logistics |
| In development | should consider the support parameters in the development stage: the analysis of operation for cost-effectiveness informs the initial design and the plan of spare parts and scheduled servicing, within the suitability requirements and the supply chains, and the maintenance needs enter the definition of the system requirements |
| Operational logistics | shall perform operational logistics |
| Its aim | should keep the system and its enabling systems configured consistently throughout operation, with the material and resources needed available in the quantity, quality, place and time needed |
| Handling | shall perform the packaging, handling, storage and transportation needed over the life cycle |
| Its reach | should cover in it the system, its elements and their replacements, in support of the aims of integration and transition |
| Replenishment | shall confirm that logistics keeps the levels of replenishment at which the stored elements meet the repair rates and the scheduled servicing |
| Spare elements | should monitor the quality and suitability of the spare elements, their transportation and their integrity in long-term storage |
| Personnel | should have personnel acquired, trained and certified where needed, to keep the number and the proficiency of the operators |
| Maintainability | shall confirm that the maintainability requirements of the system are planned, resourced and implementable |
| Readiness | should provide by logistics the operational readiness of the system: personnel, supplies, support equipment, technical data, training, equipment and computing resources, facilities |
