# Maintenance results

## Managing maintenance and logistics results

Outcomes served: data recorded, elements available.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Results | shall record the results of maintenance and logistics and each anomaly met |
| Anomalies | should name each anomaly by its source: the maintenance strategy, the enabling systems, the execution of maintenance and logistics, the definition of the system |
| Root causes | should leave the analysis of the root cause of an anomaly, the corrective actions, the improvements and the lessons to the project assessment and control process |
| Trends | shall, at the period the maintenance strategy sets, identify and record the trends in incidents, problems and maintenance and logistics actions |
| Data | shall record the failure data and the other operational data, the costs included |
| Their audience | should communicate the trends to the operation and maintenance personnel and to other projects that create or use similar systems |
| Tracking | should have the reporting of incidents and problems, with the actions taken, tracked by incident management under the quality assurance process |
| Baselines | shall maintain the key information items selected for baselines |
| Their management | should have the configuration management process establish and maintain the baselines and configuration items, typically the maintenance and life cycle support plans |
| Satisfaction | shall, at the period the maintenance strategy sets, monitor customer satisfaction with the support and the maintenance of the system and record the data |
| Its data | should pass the customer satisfaction data to the quality management process |
