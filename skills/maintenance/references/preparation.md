## Preparing for maintenance

### The strategy and the procedures

Outcomes served: elements available, needs reported.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Strategy | shall define the maintenance strategy: the management of the modifications and of the migration of products by the maintenance requirements, the release strategy and any warranty obligations |
| What is missing | should propose what a recorded strategy lacks, and present it with the output of the work for a decision |
| Plans and procedures | shall develop, record and carry out the maintenance plans and procedures - the release plan and the installation and servicing plan among them - and the product release strategy they follow, from the release plan and the product release strategy of the project planning process |
| Problem reports | shall define the procedures for receiving, recording and tracking problem reports and the users' modification requests and for feedback to the users |
| Configuration management | shall conduct configuration management for the modifications of the existing system, or establish a link with the process that conducts it |

The agent should define in the maintenance strategy, also called the maintenance concept, the
approaches, dates, resources and conditions of corrective and preventive maintenance by the
requirements of functional suitability. The agent should include in the strategy, as a rule, each
part the table names.

| The maintenance strategy carries |
|---|
| corrective and preventive strategies that sustain the services in the operational environment |
| scheduled preventive actions that reduce the likelihood of failure without loss of functions or impact on normal work, such as a suspension or a limitation of the services |
| logistics for the whole life cycle, in acquisition and in operation |
| the number, types, location and storage conditions of replacement elements, their replacement rates, storage history and refresh frequency |
| the means of countering counterfeit elements |
| the skills and training of the maintenance personnel, with the legal requirements on health, safety, security and the environment |
| maintenance measures that give insight into the levels of performance, efficiency and effectiveness |

The agent should consider in the strategy reliability-centred maintenance - identifying the causes
of failures by failure modes, effects and criticality analysis and by fault tree analysis, and a
program of cost-effective tasks that preserve the important functions - and condition-based
maintenance, which increases reliability and reduces downtime.

### The constraints and the enabling systems

Outcomes served: constraints identified, enabling systems available.

| Step | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Constraints | shall identify the constraints of maintenance on the system and pass them to the system/software requirements definition, architecture definition and design definition processes for inclusion |
| Their sources | should look for the constraints in the reuse of existing enabling systems of maintenance, the reuse of replaced elements, the replenishment of stocks, and maintenance at a special location or in a special environment |
| Trade-offs | shall identify the trade-offs under which the system, with its maintenance and logistics actions, is affordable, operable, supportable and sustainable |
| Their evaluation | should have the system analysis and decision management processes perform the evaluations and the decision support for them |
| Enabling systems | shall identify and plan the enabling systems and services maintenance needs, their requirements and interfaces included, and pass those requirements to the infrastructure management process |
| Access | shall obtain or acquire access to the enabling systems and services to be used |
| Suitability | should have the validation process confirm objectively that an enabling system achieves its intended use |
