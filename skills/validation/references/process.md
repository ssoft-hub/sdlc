# The validation process

| Name | Statement | Observable as |
|---|---|---|
| purpose | objective evidence is provided that the system, when in use, fulfils the stakeholder requirements, achieving its intended use in its intended operational environment, and that the requirements for the specific intended use of the software work product are fulfilled; validation gives confidence that the system achieves its purpose in its operational conditions, is accepted by the stakeholders, and passes the anomalies it finds to the technical process where they were introduced; it shows that the right product is built, as verification shows that the product is built right | - |
| criteria defined | the validation criteria for the stakeholder requirements and for every work product required are defined | the method and the criteria of each validation action recorded |
| suitability confirmed | the suitability of the services of the system the stakeholders require is confirmed | the conclusion of the examination of the results, accepted by the stakeholders |
| constraints identified | the validation constraints that influence the requirements, the architecture or the design are identified | the constraints recorded and passed to the stakeholder needs and requirements definition process |
| validation performed | the system or a system element is validated; the validation strategy is developed and implemented and the required validation actions are performed | the strategy and the plan recorded, the procedures recorded with their results |
| enabling systems available | the enabling systems or services validation needs are available | access obtained to each enabling system planned |
| results recorded | the validation results and the anomalies are recorded, and the problems identified and recorded | the validation results, the problem records |
| evidence provided | objective evidence is provided that the realized system satisfies the stakeholder needs and requirements and that the software work products are suitable for their intended use, and the results are made available to the customer and the other stakeholders | the validation data provided, the results made available by the procedure for forwarding reports |
| traceability established | traceability of the validated system elements is established | the traceability record of the validated elements |
