# Validation under an agreement or with independence

## Acts of this situation

| Act | An explicit request does not decide it while | Tasks still due on an explicit request |
|---|---|---|
| selecting an independent validation organization | - | Independent organization |

## Preparing for validation

Outcomes served: validation performed.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Who validates | may have the supplier, the acquirer or the acquirer's agent perform validation or take part in it |
| Responsibility | should take who is responsible for validation from the agreement |
| Independence | shall define, where the validation work needs a degree of organizational independence, the conditions for performing the process with that degree |
| Independent organization | shall select, where the project provides for independent validation, a qualified organization responsible for it, and ensure its independence and authority in the validation tasks |

## Performing validation

Outcomes served: validation performed, evidence provided.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Acquired products | should validate the acquired products for their intended use where the acquirer, as necessary, supplements the monitoring of the supplier with validation |
| Supplied products | should show by validation, where the project supplies under an agreement, that its software products or services and its processes fully satisfy the established requirements |
