## Preparing for validation

Outcomes served: validation performed, constraints identified, enabling systems available, evidence
provided, results recorded.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Process | shall establish, where the project provides for validation work, a validation process for the system or the software product |
| Its aim | should aim the validation strategy at the least cost, time and risk |
| Constraints on validation | shall identify the constraints that can limit the feasibility of the validation actions |
| Their kinds | should consider technical feasibility, cost, time, the availability of validation means and qualified personnel, and contractual constraints such as the mission's criticality |
| Strategy | shall define, record and implement the validation strategy: the actions with their methods, techniques and tools, the services, software or systems validated, the degree of independence, the schedule |
| What is missing | should propose what a recorded validation strategy lacks, and present it with the output of the work for a decision |
| Trade-off | should make the strategy a trade-off between the scope, the constraints and the way of performing the actions, evaluate an action considered for removal by its risks, order the strategy by priority, and cover the methods of each action and the enabling systems needed - training simulators, test benches, competent personnel, location, services |
| Updates | should update the strategy and its dates as the project proceeds, revising or rescheduling planned actions on unexpected events or an evolution of the system |
| What it confirms | should make the strategy confirm, by examination and objective evidence, that the characteristics of the software or the system correspond to the users' needs and the intended use, and that the implemented requirements are fulfilled |
| Plan | shall develop and record a validation plan: the items validated; the tasks; the resources, responsibilities and schedule; the procedure for forwarding reports to the acquirer and the other parties |
| Constraints on the system | shall identify and record the constraints the validation strategy puts on the system and pass them to the stakeholder needs and requirements definition process for inclusion in the stakeholder requirements |
| Their content | should consider constraints of accuracy, uncertainty, repeatability, validation means, measurement methods, suitability, availability and the interfaces with the enablers |
| Enabling systems | shall identify and plan the enabling systems and services of validation |
| Their requirements | should define their requirements and interfaces, and pass them to the infrastructure management process |
| Their kinds | should count among them validation equipment, simulators, test tools and environments |
| Ways | may lease, buy, develop, reuse or subcontract an enabling system, or combine these ways |
| Fitness | should confirm objectively, by the rules of `SKILL.md`, that an enabling system this or another process relies on achieves its intended use |
