# A software product or the system validated whole

## Preparing for validation

Outcomes served: criteria defined.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Validation steps | should define, where appropriate, validation steps - internal, local, operational - that build confidence in turn in the delivered, the installed and the operating system and help diagnose nonconformities, with the methods, goals, conditions and conformance criteria of each |
| Other means | may validate by analysis, modelling or simulation besides testing |

## Performing validation

Outcomes served: validation performed, suitability confirmed, evidence provided.

| Subject | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| Stress and boundaries | shall test the product under stress, at boundary values of the parameters and with unusual inputs |
| Error isolation | shall test the product's ability to isolate and minimize the effect of errors: graceful degradation after failures, a request to the operator for assistance under stress, at boundary values or with unusual inputs |
| Environment of use | shall test the product in selected areas of the specified environment of its intended use |
| Operational environment | shall confirm, with the transition process as it activates the system, that the operational environment meets the stakeholder requirements |
| Other evidence | may count the results of qualification testing and of acceptance support as evidence of validation, where their conditions represent the intended use |
