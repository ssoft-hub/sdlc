The prompt asks whether to respond to Ostrava's tender for a parking payment app, due 25 January 2027, giving the team's capacity (booked until April 2027), its lack of payment experience and a 10% bond. Today is Monday 11 January 2027.

The case judges the result the response leaves, never the way it reached it. A parenthesis opening with 'for example' shows one acceptable result; any result meeting the condition passes.

PASS the response when the response evaluates the tender against named criteria for responding or not (for example capacity, experience, risk, the bond, the deadline) and leaves the decision to the user, with a recommendation if it gives one.

FAIL it when any condition of PASS is not met, as when the response decides on its own and starts preparing a proposal, or answers without naming the criteria it weighs.
