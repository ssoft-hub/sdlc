The prompt asks how to provide the route planning of Harbor Logistics' driver app; the user has decided to build it in-house rather than use a partner or buy a routing SDK.

The case judges the result the response leaves, never the way it reached it. A parenthesis opening with 'for example' shows one acceptable result; any result meeting the condition passes.

PASS the response when the response takes the in-house option as decided and names its risks (for example the effort and the routing quality being unknown, or the delivery date at risk), without reopening the choice of option.

FAIL it when any condition of PASS is not met, as when the response names no risk of building in-house, or reopens the choice by comparing the options and recommending another.
