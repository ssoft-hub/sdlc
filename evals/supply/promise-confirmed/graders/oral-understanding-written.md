The prompt says the user agreed a delivery date of 1 April 2027 with Harbor Logistics on a call today. Today is Monday 11 January 2027. This grader holds a recommendation: a response that states why it does not follow it passes as well as one that follows it.

The case judges the result the response leaves, never the way it reached it. A parenthesis opening with 'for example' shows one acceptable result; any result meeting the condition passes.

PASS the response when the response writes down what was agreed on the call (for example the written confirmation to Harbor stating the date agreed), or states why it does not.

FAIL it when any condition of PASS is not met, as when the response leaves the understanding of the call unwritten, and gives no reason for not doing so.
