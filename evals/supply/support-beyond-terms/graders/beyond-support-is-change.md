The prompt asks to reply to Harbor Logistics' request for a free fix of an Android 16 crash after the support period ended on 31 December 2026. Today is Monday 11 January 2027.

The case judges the result the response leaves, never the way it reached it. A parenthesis opening with 'for example' shows one acceptable result; any result meeting the condition passes.

PASS the response when the response treats the fix as beyond the agreed support and offers it as a change to the agreement (for example a quote or an extension of support), presented for the user's decision, without starting it as free support.

FAIL it when any condition of PASS is not met, as when the response starts the fix as free support, or refuses without saying how the fix could be agreed.
