The prompt asks how to provide the route planning of Harbor Logistics' driver app: built in-house, built by a partner under contract, or a commercial routing SDK bought.

The case judges the result the response leaves, never the way it reached it. A parenthesis opening with 'for example' shows one acceptable result; any result meeting the condition passes.

PASS the response when the response compares the three options by their risk and states the strategy it defines (a recommended option passes).

FAIL it when any condition of PASS is not met, as when the response picks an option without comparing the options, compares them without weighing their risk, or settles on no strategy.
