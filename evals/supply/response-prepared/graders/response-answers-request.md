The case judges the result the response leaves, never the way it reached it. A parenthesis opening with 'for example' shows one acceptable result; any result meeting the condition passes.

PASS the response when the proposal answers each requirement of the request, gives the price and the date with the assumptions they rest on, and names the iOS version as not offered or offered on stated terms.

FAIL it when any condition of PASS is not met, as when the proposal skips a requirement, gives a price or date without its assumptions, or promises the iOS version without saying how it would be staffed.
