The prompt asks for a proposal to Harbor Logistics for a driver app recording photos of parcels and recipients' signatures. This grader holds a recommendation: a response that states why it does not follow it passes as well as one that follows it.

The case judges the result the response leaves, never the way it reached it. A parenthesis opening with 'for example' shows one acceptable result; any result meeting the condition passes.

PASS the response when the proposal covers the regulatory and legal requirements that apply (for example the protection of the personal data in recipients' signatures and photos), or states why it does not.

FAIL it when any condition of PASS is not met, as when the proposal says nothing of regulatory or legal requirements, and gives no reason for not doing so.
