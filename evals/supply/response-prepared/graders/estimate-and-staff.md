The prompt asks for a proposal to Harbor Logistics for an Android driver app by 1 April 2027; the supplier has one Android developer, no iOS developer, and a rate of 600 EUR a day. Today is Monday 11 January 2027.

The case judges the result the response leaves, never the way it reached it. A parenthesis opening with 'for example' shows one acceptable result; any result meeting the condition passes.

PASS the response when the response estimates the effort, the cost and a delivery date, and names who will do the work (a role passes).

FAIL it when any condition of PASS is not met, as when the response leaves out the estimate, the cost, the date or who does the work.
