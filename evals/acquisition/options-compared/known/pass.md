| Option | Risk | Cost | Benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Write it ourselves | layout bugs, fonts, long tail of PDF quirks | weeks of work, ongoing upkeep | full control, no new dependency |
| Open-source library | licence and maintenance of a third party | days to integrate | proven rendering, free |
| Hosted service | data leaves our system, vendor lock-in | per-document fees | no rendering code to keep |

My recommendation is the library, but this is your choice: which option do you take?
