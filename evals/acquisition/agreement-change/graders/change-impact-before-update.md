The prompt asks to update the agreement with Northwind Apps for its proposal to add an Android version for 25,000 EUR more, with no Android date and nothing signed.

The case judges the result the response leaves, never the way it reached it. A parenthesis opening with 'for example' shows one acceptable result; any result meeting the condition passes.

PASS the response when the response evaluates the impact of the change on the cost, the dates, the quality and the plans of the project, and treats the change as made only once both parties agree it and the agreement records it (for example a proposed amendment awaiting both signatures).

FAIL it when any condition of PASS is not met, as when the response rewrites the agreement as if the change were in force, or does not evaluate its impact on the cost, the dates, the quality or the plans.
